# 🛡️ What If Directors V05: The Courier and The Charioteer
### Model 4: The Mortal Eyewitnesses — Sudeva & Daruka (*Faith, Dust & Wheels*)

> *"Gods ride upon clouds, and kings ride upon golden thrones. But the world is moved by two ordinary men: an old scholar who ran until his feet bled to deliver a letter, and a driver who held the reins while the sky burned."*

---

## 🎬 Executive Overview

**Version 05 (V05)** adapts **Model 4: The Courier and The Charioteer Parallel** from the *Parallels* architecture.

This directory explores how master directors ground the epic saga of **Rukmini Kalyanam** through the **eyes, sweat, and sacrifices of mortal working men**:
- **Sudeva**: The Brahmin runner who sacrifices his physical body to deliver Rukmini's seven verses across 300 miles of enemy territory.
- **Daruka**: The master charioteer whose mechanical precision and driving genius turn Krishna's will into physical reality.
