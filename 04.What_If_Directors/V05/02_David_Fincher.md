## 2. Signature Screenplay Excerpt

```
INT. DWARKA AUDIENCE HALL - NIGHT (FINCHER GRADE)

A basin of steaming water sits on the cold stone floor. 
KRISHNA kneels, sleeves rolled up. 

SUDEVA sits on a wooden stool, trembling from shock. 
His feet are swollen, purplish-black, covered in caked mud and blood.

Krishna gently lowers the old man's foot into the warm water. 
Sudeva gasps from the sharp pain, then relaxes.

SUDEVA
My Lord... the letter...

KRISHNA
The letter is safe, Sudeva. Let the water work.

DARUKA stands by the pillar, examining the bamboo cylinder. 
He unscrews the cap with surgical care.

DARUKA
Wax seal is intact. No moisture ingress. 
Seven leaves. Saffron gallnut ink. 
He made three hundred miles in forty-six hours. 
Humanly impossible.

KRISHNA
(wiping Sudeva's heel with clean linen)
Faith is not a biological calculation, Daruka.
```
