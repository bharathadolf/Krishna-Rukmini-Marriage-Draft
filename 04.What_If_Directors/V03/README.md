# 👑 What If Directors V03: The Two Princes Mirror
### Model 2: The Psychological Foil Parallel — Krishna vs. Rukmi (*Dwarka vs. Bhojakata*)

> *"Two princes left their ancestral thrones to build new cities upon the frontier. One built an empire of white marble on the boundless ocean to shelter the weak; the other built an iron fortress in the dust to nurse his wounded pride."*

---

## 🎬 Executive Overview

**Version 03 (V03)** explores how cinema's most visionary directors would adapt **Model 2: The Two Princes Mirror (Krishna vs. Rukmi)** from the *Parallels* architecture.

Instead of reducing Prince Rukmi to a two-dimensional mustache-twirling villain, this directory explores him as the **dark psychological shadow and tragic foil to Prince Krishna**:
- **Two young royal heirs** fleeing or leaving their ancestral seats.
- **Two opposing responses to Jarasandha's imperial threat**: Selfless evacuation (*Ranachod*) vs. cynical collaboration.
- **Two brand-new cities founded on opposing principles**: Dwarka (City of open sea, liberty, and devotion) vs. Bhojakata (Garrison of spite, isolation, and bruised ego).
- **The psychological climax**: The Shaving of Rukmi—the death of his ego rather than his body.
