# 👑 V03: The Two Princes Mirror — Unified Sequences Analysis
### Krishna vs. Rukmi: The Psychological Foil Parallel Across the 150-Minute Feature

> *"Two princes left their ancestral thrones to build new cities upon the frontier. One built an empire of white marble on the boundless ocean to shelter the weak; the other built an iron fortress in the dust to nurse his wounded pride."*

---

## 1. Executive Architectural Blueprint

Version 03 (V03) explores the adaptation of the **Rukmini Kalyanam / Rukmini Haran** through **Parallel Model 2: The Two Princes Mirror (Krishna vs. Rukmi)**.

In this model, the entire narrative engine is powered by the psychological, philosophical, and moral contrast between two young royal founders:
1. **Prince Krishna of Dwarka**: The selfless leader who fled Mathura (*Ranachod*) to preserve his people, renounced royal arrogance, and built Dwarka as a sanctuary of liberty and joy.
2. **Prince Rukmi of Vidarbha**: The status-obsessed heir who usurped his righteous father Bhishmaka, commodified his sister Rukmini for 30,000 Chedi spears, and founded Bhojakata out of bitter spite after his humiliating defeat.
