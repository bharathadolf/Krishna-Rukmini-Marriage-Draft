# 💥 What If Michael Bay Directed "EMPIRE COLLAPSE: TOTAL SUBJUGATION"

> *"They've got a mechanized division, three hundred siege engines, and an entire coalition army. We've got four horses and zero fear. Move out!"*

---

## 1. The Bayhem Vision: Apex Special-Ops vs. Totalitarian War Machine

Michael Bay envisions **The Sacred Bond vs. Imperial Vice** as a **mega-scale tactical action spectacular**:
- Jarasandha's coalition is a high-tech ancient war machine with massive iron siege towers, armored combat elephants, and rocket-boosted ballistas.
- Krishna is an apex Tier-1 special-forces commander executing an impossible tactical extraction right from the center of an enemy-controlled fortress city.

---

## 2. Signature Bayhem Set Pieces

1. **The Fortress Siege Inspection**: An epic low-angle 360-degree camera spin around Shishupala and Rukmi inspecting 100,000 armored soldiers under a blazing orange-teal sunset.
2. **The Extraction Firestorm**: Krishna’s tactical war rig detonates fuel depots, vaults over burning barricades, and outruns explosive arrow barrages in high-speed slow motion.
