## 🎬 Executive Overview

**Version 04 (V04)** adapts **Model 3: The Sacred Bond vs. Imperial Vice Parallel** from the *Parallels* architecture.

This directory explores how cinema's master filmmakers transform the narrative of **Rukmini Kalyanam / Rukmini Haran** into a **deep geopolitical thriller and socio-political counterpoint**:
- **The Imperial Vice**: The totalitarian coalition of Jarasandha, Shishupala, and Rukmi—military hegemony, commodification of women, and 86 captive kings chained in Girivraja.
- **The Sacred Bond**: Sovereign choice, spiritual communion, and divine liberty represented by Krishna and Rukmini.
