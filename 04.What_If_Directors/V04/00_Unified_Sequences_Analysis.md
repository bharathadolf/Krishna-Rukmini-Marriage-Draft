# ⚖️ V04: The Sacred Bond vs. Imperial Vice — Unified Sequences Analysis
### Socio-Political Counterpoint: Sovereign Devotion vs. The Autocratic War Machine

> *"In the dark palace of Kundina, an empire was traded for thirty thousand spears and a chest of gold. In the inner chamber, a princess carved seven lines on a palm leaf and called upon the God of all worlds to overthrow their empire."*

---

## 1. Executive Architectural Blueprint

Version 04 (V04) adapts **Parallel Model 3: The Sacred Bond vs. Imperial Vice Parallel**.

This model frames the film as a **grand geopolitical thriller and socio-political counterpoint** pitting two irreconcilable visions of human existence and power against each other:
1. **The Imperial Vice (The Chedi-Magadha Axis)**: Totalitarian hegemony, imperial conquest, women commodified as diplomatic leverage, eighty-six captive kings chained in Jarasandha's dungeons in Girivraja, and brute military coercion.
2. **The Sacred Bond (The Path of Devotion & Freedom)**: Sovereign choice (*Swayamvara*), mutual spiritual recognition, the republican council of Dwarka, and Rukmini's seven-verse letter as an act of revolutionary faith.
