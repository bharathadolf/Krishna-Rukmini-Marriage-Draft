## 1. The Bayhem Vision: Twin Spec-Ops Combat Juggernaut

Michael Bay frames Krishna and Balarama as the **ultimate dual combat force**:
- Krishna represents airborne/precision strike capability (high-speed chariot, pinpoint archery).
- Balarama represents mechanized heavy artillery (divine plow, thunderous ground pounds, shockwaves).
