## 🎬 Executive Overview

**Version 06 (V06)** adapts **Model 5: The Brotherhood Divergence Parallel** from the *Parallels* architecture.

This directory explores how master filmmakers stage the epic story of **Rukmini Kalyanam** through the **contrasting prisms of brotherhood, sibling psychology, and family honor**:
- **The Yadava Brotherhood**: Krishna and Balarama—unconditional trust, mutual devotion, and intuitive military synergy.
- **The Vidarbha Dynasty**: Rukmi and his four younger brothers (Rukmaratha, Rukmabahu, Rukmakesha, Rukmamali)—toxic dominance, fear, and eventual fraternal disintegration.
