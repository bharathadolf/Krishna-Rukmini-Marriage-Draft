# 🏛️ What If Zack Snyder Directed "THE COVENANT OF SONS"
### Model 5: The Brotherhood Divergence Parallel

> *"They were not merely brothers of mortal blood. They were the eternal coils of Shesha and the golden light of Narayana, descending into the crucible of time to balance the scales of existence."*

---

## 1. The Snyder Vision: Mythic Duality of the Divine Avatars

Zack Snyder frames **The Brotherhood Divergence** as an **epic deific symphony**:
- Krishna is Narayana; Balarama is Sankarshana (the cosmic serpent Ananta Shesha). Their bond is older than the cosmos itself.
- In contrast, the five sons of Bhishmaka are the tragic symbols of mortal bloodlines tearing themselves apart.
