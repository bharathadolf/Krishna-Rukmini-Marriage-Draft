# ⚔️ V06: The Brotherhood Divergence — Unified Sequences Analysis
### Fraternal Dynasties: Mutual Trust vs. Hierarchical Tyranny Across the 150-Minute Feature

> *"A true brother stands behind you not because you command him, but because your honor is his own. A tyrant brother stands upon his brothers' shoulders and wonders why he is always alone when he falls."*

---

## 1. Executive Architectural Blueprint

Version 06 (V06) adapts **Parallel Model 5: The Brotherhood Divergence Parallel**.

This model anchors the feature film around the **dynamics of brotherhood and family systems**:
1. **The Yadava Brotherhood (Krishna & Balarama)**: Built on mutual devotion, shared trials (from Gokula cowherds to Mathura wars), unconditional trust, and complementary genius—Krishna's strategic grace and Balarama's unstoppable martial thunder (*Hala* and *Gada*).
2. **The Vidarbha Brotherhood (Rukmi and the 4 Younger Brothers)**: Rukmaratha, Rukmabahu, Rukmakesha, and Rukmamali living under the suffocating shadow of Rukmi's narcissistic tyranny, silenced by fear and forced to validate their eldest brother's disastrous decisions.
