## 1. Executive Architectural Blueprint

Version 08 (V08) adapts **Model 7: The Grand Screenplay Recommendation (The Master 150-Minute Synthesis)** from the *Parallels* architecture.

This model is the **culmination of the entire project**: it synthesizes all six previous parallel dimensions into an exact, production-ready **16-sequence, 150-minute theatrical screenplay blueprint**:
1. **The Cosmic Axis (Model 1)**: The prologue of Ksheera Sagara Madhanam and the epilogue of Kurukshetra frame the entire narrative.
2. **The Psychological Axis (Model 2)**: Krishna and Rukmi mirror each other as rival founders (Dwarka vs. Bhojakata).
3. **The Geopolitical Axis (Model 3)**: Sovereign devotion clashes against Jarasandha's totalitarian empire and the 86 captive kings.
4. **The Mortal Axis (Model 4)**: The sweat and blood of Sudeva and the driving genius of Daruka ground the spectacle in human tears.
5. **The Fraternal Axis (Model 5)**: Krishna and Balarama's loving brotherhood contrasts with Rukmi's toxic dominance over his four brothers.
6. **The Ticking Clock Axis (Model 6)**: The 72-hour accelerating countdown propels the second act into a breathless high-noon climax.
