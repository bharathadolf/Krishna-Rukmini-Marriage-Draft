# 🩸 What If Quentin Tarantino Directed "THE SEVEN VERSES, THE SERPENT & THE SACRED MASSACRE"
### A Chapter-Based Mythological Pulp Epic Across Three Yugas

> *"See, back in the Satya Yuga, the titans thought they could muscle the Goddess into marrying them. Then in the Dwapara Yuga, Prince Rukmi thought he could sell his sister for thirty thousand spears. And by the time everybody met up at Kurukshetra, nobody was doing any more talking."*

Tarantino solves the "short story" problem by structuring the film around **The Five Legendary Chapters of Cosmic Retribution**, tying the birth of Goddess Lakshmi directly to the highway abduction of Rukmini and the bloodbath of Kurukshetra:

## 2. Visual Grammar & Cinematography (Robert Richardson Aesthetic)

- **Film Stock**: 70mm Ultra Panavision. Deep, saturated Technicolor palette.
- **The Tarantino Signature Shots**:
  - **The Snap-Zoom (Crash-Zoom)**: Rapid, whip-speed zooms straight into characters' eyes when tensions reach a boiling point.
  - **The Trunk Shot**: Low-angle camera placed inside the floorboards of the chariot looking up at Krishna and Daruka as they check the axle-grease and leather harnesses.
  - **The Blood Fountain**: In the Kurukshetra flash-forward, whenever Shishupala’s head is severed or a warrior falls, geysers of bright theatrical crimson spray across carved marble pillars.
  - **Yellow Retro Title Cards**: Bold, vintage 1970s typography introducing every major warrior:
    - `LORD BALARAMA — THE HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION OF THE YADAVAS`
    - `PRINCE RUKMI — THE LOUDEST MOUTH IN VIDARBHA`
    - `SHISHUPALA — THE MAN WITH ONE HUNDRED PARDONS AND ZERO BRAINS`

## 3. Soundtrack & Sound Design (Ennio Morricone + 70s Bombay Funk + Raw Dhol)

- **The Crate-Digging Soundtrack**:
  - Spaghetti western acoustic guitars and lonely whistling (reminiscent of *The Good, the Bad and the Ugly*).
  - 1970s psychedelic sitar funk, wah-wah guitar grooves, and heavy Indian brass (R.D. Burman energy meets *Django Unchained*).
  - Thundering Punjabi dhol beats accelerating like machine-gun fire during the chariot pursuit.
- **Sound Effects**:
  - Exaggerated whip-cracks that sound like dynamite blasts.
  - The loud, metallic *SCHWING-CHUNK!* of bronze swords drawn from wooden scabbards.
  - The high-pitched, terrifying buzz of the Sudarshana Chakra spinning at 10,000 RPM.

## 4. Sample Screenplay Excerpt: The Standoff Across Time

```text
EXT. AMBIKA TEMPLE - STEPS - HIGH NOON (CHAPTER 3)

ULTRA PANAVISION 70. 

A dead-quiet standoff. The desert wind WHISTLES a lonely Morricone tune.

RUKMINI stands at the top of forty marble stairs, draped in blood-red silk. 
Behind her, CAPTAIN VIRA and forty Chedi guards grip their scimitars.

At the bottom of the stairs sits a single lightweight war chariot. 
Four massive white stallions pawing red dust.

Leaning against the gilded wheel-hub is KRISHNA. 
Peacock feather tilted in His turban. 
He’s casually polishing a golden betel-nut cutter against His sleeve.

CRASH ZOOM on Captain Vira’s sweaty face.

CAPTAIN VIRA
Step away from the carriage, Yadava! 
Prince Rukmi has twenty thousand men coming over that ridge!

Krishna doesn't look up from His betel-nut cutter.

KRISHNA
Now see, Vira, that sounds real intimidating on parchment. 
Twenty thousand men. Lots of spears. Lots of noise. 
Problem is, I got a letter here in my sash from the lady on the stairs. 
And she didn't ask for a debate. She asked for an extraction.

CRASH ZOOM on Rukmini’s eyes. 
She smirks. Her fingers touch the silver dagger hidden in her sleeve.

FLASH-CUT TO:

EXT. INDRAPRASTHA - RAJASUYA SACRIFICE - DAY (CHAPTER 4 FLASH-FORWARD)

A grand royal pavilion. A thousand kings. 
SHISHUPALA stands in the center of the hall, red-faced, screaming at Krishna.

SHISHUPALA
You cattle-thief! You cowherd! You stolen-butter outlaw! 
That’s ninety-nine insults! You want to hear number one hundred?!

ON KRISHNA: 
Sitting on the golden throne, smiling His lazy, terrifying smile.

KRISHNA
Take your time, Shishupala. Savor it. 
Because once you hit one hundred and one, the conversation is over.

SHISHUPALA
(screaming at the top of his lungs)
YOU ARE A CUR! A COWARD! AND A BASTARD!

Krishna smiles. 
His index finger FLICKS.

THE SUDARSHANA CHAKRA spins off His finger with the deafening sound of a circular saw. 
*SHINGGGGGGG-THUMP!*

Shishupala’s head flies off in a spray of bright crimson, 
landing square in the center of the sacrificial fruit platter.

FLASH-CUT TO:

EXT. KSHEERA SAGARA - SATYA YUGA (PROLOGUE FLASHBACK)

From the boiling milk sea, MAHALAKSHMI rises upon the diamond lotus. 
Fifty Asura warlords draw blood-stained scimitars, howling in lust.

Lakshmi rolls her eyes, gathers her skirts, walks past every single one of them, 
and slaps the garland right onto Lord Vishnu’s neck.

LAKSHMI
(deadpan)
Took you long enough, cowboy.

FLASH-CUT BACK TO:

EXT. AMBIKA TEMPLE - NOON (CHAPTER 3)

Captain Vira screams:

CAPTAIN VIRA
SHOOT THE COWHERD!

Krishna’s arm WHIPS forward. 
His silver Chakra flies through the air, ricocheting off four pillars:
*SHING! CRACK! SHING! CRACK!*
It cleanly slices the bowstrings of every single archer on the steps.

Before the snapped strings hit the floor:
Krishna looks up at Rukmini.

KRISHNA
Rukmini! You want to ride, or you want to give a speech?!

Rukmini hoists her scarlet skirts and SPRINTS down the marble steps.

She leaps onto the chariot bed. 
Krishna catches her, swings her behind Him, and CRACKS the reins.

*KABOOM!*

The chariot tears into the canyon, leaving forty guards choking on red dust.

CUT TO RETRO TITLE CARD:
CHAPTER 5: THE SHAVING OF PRINCE RUKMI
```

## 5. Concluding Scene: The Barber of Bhojakata
The climax ends on the highway to Dwarka. 
Krishna has Rukmi pinned to the ground with a sandal on his chest. 
Rukmi is shrieking like Calvin Candie in *Django Unchained*:
*"Kill me! An Aryan prince does not live in shame!"*

Krishna pulls a gleaming silver straight-razor from His boot.
*"See, Rukmi, killing you means your sister cries at dinner. I don't like my bride crying at dinner. 
So here’s the deal: You’re gonna walk out of here alive. But you’re gonna look like a plucked rooster."*

Krishna shaves half his head and half his mustache in three stylish, comedic swipes.
Balarama leans over his chariot, takes a massive gulp of palm wine, and laughs so hard he almost drops his iron plowshare.
