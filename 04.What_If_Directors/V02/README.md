# 🌌 What If Directors V2: The Multi-Temporal Cosmic Epic
### Bridging *Ksheera Sagara Madhanam*, *Rukmini Kalyanam*, and *The Mahabharata War*

---

## 🎬 Executive Overview: Solving the "Short Film" Dilemma

Adapting only the abduction of Rukmini from the Ambika temple risks shrinking one of Indian mythology's greatest sagas into a simple 30-minute extraction sequence. 

**Version 2 (V2)** completely reconceives the narrative into a **full-scale, 2.5-to-3-hour feature film** by weaving **Three Interlocking Timelines**:
1. **The Cosmic Origin (Satya Yuga)**: *Ksheera Sagara Madhanam* (The Churning of the Ocean of Milk) — Establishing the eternal cosmic vow between Vishnu and Lakshmi and the first sovereign choice (*Swayamvara*).
2. **The Mortal Crucible (Dwapara Yuga Present)**: *Rukmini Kalyanam / Rukmini Haran* — The ticking-clock rescue, the 7-sloka letter, the Ambika temple heist, and the defeat of the allied kings.
3. **The Prophetic Climax (Future Dwapara / Kurukshetra)**: *The Mahabharata War* — The cosmic consequences of human hubris: Krishna as Parthasarathi, the revelation of Vishwaroopa, Shishupala's decapitation at the Rajasuya Yajna, and the fall of the old world order.
