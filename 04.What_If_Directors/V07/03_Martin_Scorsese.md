# 🍷 What If Martin Scorsese Directed "72 HOURS TO JUDGMENT DAY"
### Model 6: The Structural Convergence Parallel

> *"The last seventy-two hours felt like a bad coke high. Everybody was screaming, everybody was double-crossing each other, and the wedding was turning into an execution."*

---

## 1. The Scorsese Vision: Manic Nervous Breakdown Tempo

Scorsese captures the ticking clock through **wild, manic camera energy**:
- Rapid whip-pans, freeze-frames, jump cuts, and characters sweating through their expensive silks.
- The wedding day feels like a chaotic, out-of-control carnival about to collapse into violence.
