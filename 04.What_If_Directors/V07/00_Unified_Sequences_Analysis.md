## 1. Executive Architectural Blueprint

Version 07 (V07) adapts **Parallel Model 6: The Structural Convergence Parallel**.

This model transforms the entire 150-minute narrative into a **breathless, accelerating real-time ticking clock thriller**:
- **72 Hours Remaining**: Four independent narrative strands begin at different speeds and locations across ancient India.
- **24 Hours Remaining**: The cross-cutting tempo doubles; threats escalate on all fronts.
- **1 Hour Remaining**: Cross-cutting becomes hyper-rapid; minutes and seconds are counted down.
- **High Noon (12:00 PM)**: All four strands violently slam together at the Ambika Temple gate in a synchronized explosion of action.
