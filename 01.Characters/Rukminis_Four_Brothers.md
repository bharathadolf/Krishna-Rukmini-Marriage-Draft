# Rukmini's Four Younger Brothers

## Role in the Story
**Minor Supporting Characters** — The four younger brothers of Rukmini, sons of King Bhishmaka.

## The Four Brothers

### 1. Rukmaratha
- Second eldest son of King Bhishmaka
- Unlike Rukmi, is more aligned with his father's gentler disposition
- Mentioned among Rukmini's siblings in the Bhagavata Purana

### 2. Rukmabahu
- Third son of King Bhishmaka
- A prince of Vidarbha with warrior training
- Mentioned in the lineage of Bhishmaka's sons

### 3. Rukmakesa
- Fourth son of King Bhishmaka
- A prince of Vidarbha
- Mentioned alongside his brothers in Puranic accounts

### 4. Rukmamali
- Fifth son of King Bhishmaka (youngest of the five brothers)
- A prince of Vidarbha
- Mentioned in the Bhagavata Purana's account of the family

## Collective Role
- All four younger brothers are significantly more sympathetic to Krishna than their eldest brother Rukmi
- They do not oppose Rukmini's love for Krishna
- Their individual roles in the wedding story are minor, but they form part of the royal family's complete picture
- Contrast with Rukmi — while he dominates the political agenda, the younger brothers represent the softer, less bellicose side of the Vidarbha royal family

## Note
The primary antagonist among the brothers is Rukmi alone. The four younger brothers are secondary figures in the main narrative and play no direct active role in either facilitating or opposing the marriage.

## Source
*Bhagavata Purana*, Canto 10, Chapter 52
