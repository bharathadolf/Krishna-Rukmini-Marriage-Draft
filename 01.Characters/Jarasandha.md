# Jarasandha

## Role in the Story
**Allied Antagonist** — Powerful King of Magadha and the supreme commander of the coalition army that pursues Krishna after the abduction.

## Description
Jarasandha is the mighty King of Magadha and one of the most powerful rulers of his era. He is the father-in-law of the slain tyrant King Kansa (whose daughters were Asti and Prapti, wives of Kansa). After Krishna killed Kansa, Jarasandha became Krishna's most relentless enemy.

## Role in Krishna-Rukmini Marriage
- Allied with Rukmi and Shishupala — all united in their enmity toward Krishna
- After Krishna abducts Rukmini, Jarasandha leads the massive coalition army in pursuit
- Commands an enormous force attempting to stop Krishna and recapture Rukmini
- Is defeated and routed by the Yadava forces under Krishna and Balarama
- Retreats but does not give up his enmity (he attacked Mathura 17 times prior to this and continued harassment afterward)

## Key Traits
- Enormously powerful — nearly invincible warrior king
- Deeply motivated by personal vendetta (avenging Kansa's death)
- Persistent — never gives up despite repeated defeats at Krishna's hands
- A great military strategist and conqueror

## Later Fate
- Eventually killed by Bhima at Krishna's direction during the Rajasuya Yagna of Yudhishthira
  — Bhima tears him apart with his bare hands following Krishna's tactical guidance

## Source
*Bhagavata Purana*, Canto 10, Chapters 52–54
*Mahabharata*, Sabha Parva (for his death)
