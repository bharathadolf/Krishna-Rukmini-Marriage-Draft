# Sudeva — The Brahmin Messenger

## Role in the Story
**Trusted Messenger** — The Brahmin sent by Rukmini to deliver her secret letter to Lord Krishna in Dwarka.

## Description
Sudeva is a trusted Brahmin in the court of King Bhishmaka of Vidarbha. He is chosen by Princess Rukmini as her secret envoy to carry her heartfelt letter to Lord Krishna.

## Role in Krishna-Rukmini Marriage
- Chosen by Rukmini as someone trustworthy and wise enough to carry out a highly sensitive mission
- Travels from Vidarbha to Dwarka and gains an audience with Lord Krishna
- Delivers Rukmini's letter — a beautifully composed message expressing her love, devotion, and plea for rescue
- Krishna receives him with great respect and hospitality, befitting the messenger of his beloved
- After reading the letter, Krishna immediately decides to travel to Vidarbha
- Sudeva returns to Kundina (Vidarbha) carrying Krishna's assurance to Rukmini

## Significance
- Represents the role of a faithful intermediary between two devotees
- His successful mission sets the entire marriage narrative in motion
- Honored by Krishna himself — reflecting the Vedic tradition of respecting the messenger as equal to the sender

## Key Traits
- Trustworthy, discreet, and wise
- Devoted to Rukmini and her well-being
- Capable of navigating the royal courts of two kingdoms under sensitive circumstances

## Source
*Bhagavata Purana*, Canto 10, Chapters 52–53
