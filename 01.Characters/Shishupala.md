# Shishupala (Sisupala)

## Role in the Story
**Rival Suitor & Antagonist** — King of Chedi and the man chosen by Rukmi to marry Rukmini.

## Description
Shishupala is the King of Chedi and a cousin of Lord Krishna (his mother Srutadevi is Krishna's father Vasudeva's sister). Despite this family connection, Shishupala is one of Krishna's fiercest and most vocal enemies.

## Background & Divine Context
- In a previous life, he was Hiranyakashipu (killed by Narasimha avatar) and later Ravana (killed by Rama avatar)
- In each incarnation, he is blessed to be liberated by the hands of Vishnu after 100 offenses
- His enmity toward Krishna is thus cosmically ordained

## Role in Krishna-Rukmini Marriage
- Chosen by Rukmi as the preferred groom for Rukmini, cementing a political alliance
- Arrives at Kundina for the grand wedding ceremony
- Is humiliated when Krishna abducts Rukmini from right under his nose
- Joins the allied army of kings (with Jarasandha, Salva, Rukmi, etc.) to chase Krishna
- Is defeated along with the coalition army by Krishna and Balarama

## Later Fate
- Continues his open enmity and insults toward Krishna for years
- Is finally killed by Krishna at the Rajasuya Yagna of Yudhishthira after uttering 100 insults — his soul merges into Krishna (moksha)

## Key Traits
- Arrogant, hot-headed, and verbally abusive toward Krishna
- A powerful and capable warrior king despite his enmity
- Ironically attains liberation (moksha) through his hatred of Krishna — a unique spiritual path called *dvesha-bhakti* (liberation through animosity)

## Source
*Bhagavata Purana*, Canto 10, Chapters 52–54 & Chapter 74 (Rajasuya Yagna)
