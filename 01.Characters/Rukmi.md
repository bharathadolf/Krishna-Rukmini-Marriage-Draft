# Rukmi

## Role in the Story
**Primary Antagonist** — Eldest brother of Rukmini and chief architect of the opposition to the Krishna-Rukmini union.

## Description
Rukmi is the eldest son of King Bhishmaka and the crown prince of Vidarbha. Unlike his gentle father, Rukmi is proud, ambitious, and politically driven. He is a close ally of King Kansa and later of Shishupala and Jarasandha.

## Role in Krishna-Rukmini Marriage
- Strongly opposes Rukmini marrying Krishna despite his father's and siblings' wishes
- Arranges for Rukmini to be wed to Shishupala, his political ally
- Organizes a grand ceremony at Kundina for the Shishupala wedding
- Rallies the allied kings (Shishupala, Jarasandha, Salva, etc.) to pursue Krishna after the abduction
- Personally gives chase to Krishna with a vast army
- Is defeated in single combat by Krishna
- Has his head partially shaved — a form of humiliation short of death — and is spared only at Rukmini's tearful plea

## Post-Marriage Fate
- Vows never to return to Vidarbha after his humiliation
- Founds his own city, Bhojakata (Bhojapura)
- Later becomes a recurring minor antagonist in the Yadava circle
- Is eventually killed by Balarama during a rigged dice game episode (Aniruddha's marriage subplot)

## Key Traits
- Prideful, stubborn, and politically ambitious
- Holds deep enmity toward Krishna throughout his life
- Represents the force of ego and worldly politics standing against divine will

## Source
*Bhagavata Purana*, Canto 10, Chapters 52–54 & later chapters
