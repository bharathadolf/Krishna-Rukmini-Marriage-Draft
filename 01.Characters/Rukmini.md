# Rukmini

## Role in the Story
**Heroine & Bride** — Princess of Vidarbha, avatar of Goddess Lakshmi, and the first and principal queen of Lord Krishna.

## Description
Rukmini is the daughter of King Bhishmaka of Vidarbha and is celebrated as the most beautiful and virtuous princess of her age. She is considered a direct incarnation of Goddess Lakshmi (Shri), the consort of Lord Vishnu.

## Role in Krishna-Rukmini Marriage
- Falls deeply in love with Krishna after hearing about his divine qualities and feats
- Opposes her brother Rukmi's plan to marry her off to Shishupala
- Composes a heartfelt, eloquent letter to Krishna expressing her love and requesting him to rescue her
- Sends the letter secretly via the trusted Brahmin messenger Sudeva
- Devises a plan — suggests that Krishna abduct her when she steps outside to visit the temple of Goddess Ambika (the only occasion a princess would be outside the palace before her wedding)
- Is abducted by Krishna and taken to Dwarka
- Becomes Krishna's *Patrani* (chief queen) and bears him a son, Pradyumna

## Divine Identity
- Avatar of Goddess Lakshmi (Shri / Mahalakshmi)
- Her union with Krishna represents the eternal cosmic union of Vishnu and Lakshmi

## Key Traits
- Exceptionally beautiful, intelligent, and devoted
- Courageous — took the bold step of writing directly to Krishna against societal norms
- Deeply spiritual — her love for Krishna was not merely romantic but rooted in devotion (bhakti)
- Witty and sharp — later Puranic stories depict lively conversations between her and Krishna

## Source
*Bhagavata Purana*, Canto 10, Chapters 52–54
