# Balarama (Balaram)

## Role in the Story
**Ally & Elder Brother** — Krishna's elder brother who accompanies and supports him throughout the mission to Vidarbha.

## Description
Balarama (also known as Baladeva or Sankarshana) is the elder brother of Lord Krishna and an avatar of Adi Sesha (the divine serpent). He is immensely powerful, wielding the plow (Hala) and the mace (Musala) as his weapons.

## Role in Krishna-Rukmini Marriage
- Accompanies Krishna to the kingdom of Vidarbha with a Yadava military escort
- Commands and protects the Yadava forces when the allied kings (Jarasandha, Shishupala's army) give chase after the abduction
- Helps repel and rout the pursuing coalition army
- Plays a key supportive role in ensuring the safe return of Krishna and Rukmini to Dwarka

## Key Traits
- Immensely strong — considered stronger than Krishna in physical combat
- Steady, loyal, and protective
- Serves as the military backbone of the Yadava clan

## Relation to the Story's Later Events
- Later kills Rukmi during a rigged dice game at the wedding of Aniruddha (Rukmini's grandson)
  — this becomes a source of grief for Rukmini

## Source
*Bhagavata Purana*, Canto 10, Chapters 52–54
