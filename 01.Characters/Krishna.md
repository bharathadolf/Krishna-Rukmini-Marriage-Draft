# Lord Krishna

## Role in the Story
**Hero & Groom** — The Supreme Personality of Godhead and central figure of the marriage story.

## Description
Lord Krishna is the eighth avatar of Lord Vishnu and the divine hero of the Bhagavata Purana. He is the ruler of Dwarka (Dwaraka), a magnificent kingdom he established for the Yadava clan.

## Role in Krishna-Rukmini Marriage
- Receives Rukmini's secret love letter delivered by the Brahmin messenger Sudeva
- Travels to the kingdom of Vidarbha (Kundina) with a military escort to rescue Rukmini
- Abducts Rukmini in the *Rakshasa* (heroic abduction) form of marriage — a tradition fit for a Kshatriya princess — right outside the temple of Goddess Ambika
- Defeats the allied army of Jarasandha and Shishupala who give chase
- Defeats and spares Rukmi (Rukmini's brother) at Rukmini's request
- Marries Rukmini in Dwarka, making her his principal queen (Patrani)

## Divine Identity
- Avatar of Lord Vishnu
- Rukmini is considered the avatar of Goddess Lakshmi — their union represents the eternal bond of Vishnu and Lakshmi

## Key Traits
- Supremely compassionate, wise, and all-powerful
- Responds to pure devotion — Rukmini's letter moved him to act immediately
- Merciful — spared Rukmi's life despite Rukmi's enmity

## Source
*Bhagavata Purana*, Canto 10, Chapters 52–54
