## 📋 PHASE 1 — STORY DEVELOPMENT

> **Output:** A locked story document before any scripting begins.

### Step 1.1 — Logline
Write a single sentence (25–30 words) summarizing the film's core conflict.

> **File to create:** `Story/01_Logline.md`
>
> **Example format:**
> *"A divine princess secretly defies her powerful brother and all royal politics to summon the one man who can rescue her — the God she loves."*

---

### Step 1.2 — Premise / Theme Statement
Define the central theme and emotional truth of the film.

> **File to create:** `Story/02_Premise_Theme.md`
>
> **Key questions to answer:**
> - What is the film ABOUT emotionally? *(e.g., devotion vs. politics)*
> - What does Rukmini want? What does she need?
> - What does Krishna's action represent spiritually?
> - What is the film's moral/philosophical argument?

---

### Step 1.3 — Genre & Tone Document
Define the cinematic genre, sub-genre, tone, and visual language.

> **File to create:** `Story/03_Genre_Tone.md`
>
> **Categories to define:**
> - Primary Genre: *(e.g., Epic Historical / Mythological Drama)*
> - Tone: *(e.g., Majestic, Devotional, Emotionally grounded)*
> - Comparable Films: *(e.g., Baahubali, Adipurush, Mughal-E-Azam, RRR)*
> - Visual Reference: Color palette, era feel, realism vs. stylization

---

### Step 1.4 — World Bible / Setting Document
Document all kingdoms, locations, time periods, social customs.

> **File to create:** `Story/04_World_Bible.md`
>
> **Locations to document:**
> - Dwarka (Krishna's Kingdom)
> - Vidarbha / Kundina (Rukmini's Kingdom)
> - Chedi (Shishupala's Kingdom)
> - Magadha (Jarasandha's Kingdom)
> - The Forest/Chase Route
> - Temple of Goddess Ambika (pivotal location)

---

### Step 1.5 — Character Arcs (Per Character)
Expand each character file into a full arc document showing transformation.

> **Files to update/create:** `Characters/*_Arc.md`
>
> **For each major character define:**
> - Starting emotional state (Act 1)
> - Midpoint shift
> - Climactic decision or action (Act 3)
> - Ending emotional state / fate
> - Internal wound / desire / need (screenwriting triangle)

## 📋 PHASE 2 — STRUCTURAL DEVELOPMENT

> **Output:** A complete structural map of the screenplay.

### Step 2.1 — Three-Act Structure Breakdown
Map the story into the classic three-act structure.

> **File to create:** `Structure/01_Three_Act_Breakdown.md`
>
> | Act | Pages | Content |
> |-----|-------|---------|
> | Act 1 — Setup | 1–25 | World, characters introduced; Rukmini's dilemma established |
> | Act 2A — Confrontation | 25–55 | Rukmini's letter; Rukmi's arrangement; Krishna sets out |
> | Midpoint | ~55 | Krishna arrives at Kundina |
> | Act 2B — Complication | 55–85 | The abduction; the chase; the battle |
> | Act 3 — Resolution | 85–110 | Defeat of Rukmi; arrival at Dwarka; the wedding |

---

### Step 2.2 — Scene-by-Scene Outline (Beat Sheet)
List every scene in order with a one-line description. This is the backbone of the screenplay.

> **File to create:** `Structure/02_Beat_Sheet.md`
>
> **Format per beat:**
> ```
> SCENE [Number] — [Location] — [INT/EXT] — [DAY/NIGHT]
> [One sentence: What happens + why it matters]
> ```
>
> **Target:** ~55–65 scenes for a 110-page screenplay

---

### Step 2.3 — Sequence Breakdown (8-Sequence Method)
Divide the film into 8 sequences of ~12–15 minutes each.

> **File to create:** `Structure/03_Sequence_Breakdown.md`
>
> | Sequence | Name | Pages |
> |----------|------|-------|
> | 1 | Status Quo — Dwarka & Vidarbha | 1–15 |
> | 2 | The Conflict — Rukmi's Plan | 15–30 |
> | 3 | The Letter — Rukmini's Plea | 30–45 |
> | 4 | Krishna's Journey | 45–55 |
> | 5 | Kundina — Day of the Wedding | 55–65 |
> | 6 | The Abduction | 65–75 |
> | 7 | The Chase & Battle | 75–90 |
> | 8 | Victory & The Wedding in Dwarka | 90–110 |

---

### Step 2.4 — Scene Cards (Index Cards Method)
Create one card per scene with: Scene #, Location, Characters, Goal, Conflict, Outcome.

> **File to create:** `Structure/04_Scene_Cards.md`

## 📋 PHASE 3 — SCREENPLAY WRITING (FIRST DRAFT)

> **Output:** A complete, unpolished first draft (~100–120 pages)

### Step 3.1 — Learn / Apply Standard Screenplay Format

All feature screenplays use **industry-standard format** (Courier 12pt, specific margins).

> **File to create:** `Screenplay/FORMAT_GUIDE.md`
>
> **Key formatting rules:**
> ```
> SCENE HEADING:    INT. PALACE OF VIDARBHA - NIGHT
> ACTION LINE:      Rukmini paces the moonlit corridor, clutching a rolled parchment.
> CHARACTER CUE:              RUKMINI
> DIALOGUE:         (reading aloud, barely a whisper)
>                   I have chosen my lord. Let the world burn.
> PARENTHETICAL:    (rising)
> TRANSITION:       CUT TO:
> ```
>
> **Recommended free tools:**
> - Final Draft (industry standard, paid)
> - WriterDuet (free tier available) — https://writerduet.com/
> - Fade In (affordable) — https://www.fadeinpro.com/
> - Fountain (plain text format, free) — https://fountain.io/

---

### Step 3.2 — Write the First Draft Scene by Scene

Follow the Beat Sheet (Step 2.2) strictly. Write each scene in screenplay format.

> **Files to create:** `Screenplay/Draft_01/Act_1.fountain`
> `Screenplay/Draft_01/Act_2A.fountain`
> `Screenplay/Draft_01/Act_2B.fountain`
> `Screenplay/Draft_01/Act_3.fountain`
>
> **First Draft Rules:**
> - Write FAST — do not edit while writing
> - Every scene must have: a GOAL → OBSTACLE → OUTCOME
> - 1 page screenplay = approx. 1 minute of screen time
> - Target: **110 pages = ~110 minutes runtime**

---

### Step 3.3 — Dialogue Pass
Once the draft is complete, go back and refine all dialogue.

> **File to create:** `Screenplay/Notes/Dialogue_Pass_Notes.md`
>
> **Key considerations:**
> - Each character must sound DISTINCT — no two characters speak alike
> - Rukmini's letter scene is the emotional centerpiece — treat it as a soliloquy
> - Sanskrit shlokas or classical references can be woven in as stylistic layer
> - Avoid over-explanation — show through action, not dialogue dumps

---

## 📋 PHASE 4 — REVISION & POLISH

> **Output:** A polished, production-ready draft

### Step 4.1 — Structure Pass (Second Draft)
Read the full draft for structural integrity. Fix pacing issues.

> **File to create:** `Screenplay/Notes/Revision_Pass_1_Structure.md`
>
> **Check:**
> - Does Act 1 end with a clear break/inciting incident?
> - Is the midpoint a true reversal?
> - Does Act 2B escalate properly?
> - Does the climax feel earned?

---

### Step 4.2 — Character Pass (Third Draft)
Read once for each MAJOR character separately.

> **File to create:** `Screenplay/Notes/Revision_Pass_2_Character.md`
>
> **For each character ask:**
> - Is their voice consistent?
> - Do their actions arise from character, not plot convenience?
> - Is their arc complete?

---

### Step 4.3 — Action / Visual Pass (Fourth Draft)
Ensure every scene is visually described and cinematically vivid.

> **File to create:** `Screenplay/Notes/Revision_Pass_3_Visual.md`
>
> **Focus on:**
> - Are action lines lean, vivid, and present-tense?
> - Are key visual moments written with cinematic specificity?
> - Unnecessary camera directions removed (directors prefer no camera direction in spec scripts)

---

### Step 4.4 — Table Read
Read the full screenplay aloud with different people voicing each character.

> **Purpose:** Catches awkward dialogue, pacing issues, and unclear scenes
> **Record observations in:** `Screenplay/Notes/Table_Read_Notes.md`

---

### Step 4.5 — Final Polish Draft (Fifth Draft)
Implement all table read notes. Final line-by-line grammar and formatting check.

> **Output File:** `Screenplay/Krishna_Rukmini_FINAL_DRAFT.pdf`

---

## 📋 PHASE 5 — BINDING & PRODUCTION PACKAGE

> **Output:** A physically bound screenplay ready for submission/presentation

### Step 5.1 — Screenplay Title Page

> **Standard format:**
> ```
>
>
>             KRISHNA & RUKMINI
>
>             Written by
>             [Your Name]
>
>
>
>
>
>
>         Based on the Bhagavata Purana
>         Canto 10, Chapters 52-54
>
>                              WGA Registration # (if applicable)
>                              [Contact Info]
> ```

---

### Step 5.2 — Export to PDF

> **Specs:**
> - Font: Courier Prime 12pt
> - Margins: Left 1.5", Right 1", Top/Bottom 1"
> - Page numbers: Top right (except title page)

---

### Step 5.3 — Physical Binding (Industry Standard)

> **Materials needed:**
> - **Brass brads (fasteners)** — 2 brads through holes 1 and 3 of a 3-hole punch
>   *(Never use all 3 holes — always skip the middle hole)*
> - **Card stock covers** — Plain front and back covers
> - **3-hole punch** — Punch all pages uniformly
> - **No spiral binding** — Industry standard is brads only

---

### Step 5.4 — Copyright & Registration

> **Steps:**
> 1. **WGA Script Registration**
>    - URL: https://www.wgawregistry.org/
>    - Cost: ~$22 (non-members) | Valid: 5 years
>
> 2. **Copyright Registration (Stronger legal protection)**
>    - URL: https://www.copyright.gov/
>    - Cost: ~$65 | Lifetime protection
