# 🎭 Parallels: Pattern-Based Story Writing Master Archive
### Transforming Character Backstories into an Epic 2-Hour 30-Minute (150-Min) Feature Film

> *"Linear storytelling narrates what happens. Parallel storytelling reveals why it was inevitable. When character lives, choices, and eras mirror each other across the screen, a simple mythological incident transforms into a world-class cinematic masterpiece."*

---

## 🎯 Executive Overview

This archive was created to solve the fundamental screenwriting challenge of adapting the **Rukmini Kalyanam / Rukmini Haran** (*Bhagavata Purana*, Canto 10, Ch. 52–54) into a **feature-length 2-hour 30-minute (150-minute) theatrical film**.

By mining the comprehensive character backstories and scene banks gathered in **`02.Character stories`**, this directory provides **six distinct structural models of parallel storytelling**, culminating in a **master unified 150-minute screenplay blueprint**.
