The complete, standalone mythological saga of Ksheera Sagara Madhanam (Samudra Manthana) across 22 dramatic scenes.
FADE IN:
EXT. AMARAVATI - CELESTIAL PROMENADE - DAY#1#
The sky is a radiant expanse of molten amber and spun silk. Floating crystalline spires pierce the upper stratosphere, connected by arches of solid rainbow.
Thousands of DEVA COURTIERS and APSARAS line the golden highway, cheering. Petals of Parijata flowers rain through the warm air, turning to sparkling embers before touching the flawless marble flagstones.
ATOP A COLOSSAL ELEPHANT—
AIRAVATA. Four ivory tusks curving toward the sun. A mountain of muscle draped in golden armor and silk brocade.
SEATED IN THE JEWELED HOWDAH—
INDRA (30s in immortal appearance). Radiance pouring from every pore. Clad in diamond breastplate and emerald-crested crown. His fingers casually roll a gold cup of Soma wine. His eyes are heavy with boredom and absolute supremacy.
AT THE ELEPHANT’S FLANK—
MATALI, the celestial charioteer, walks beside the tusker, holding the reins of Indra's battle car.
INDRA
(calling down, smirking)
Matali! Tell me—in all the records of the sages, has any king ever held a realm so peaceful, so entirely devoid of threat?
MATALI
(bowing slightly, measured)
None, My Lord. The Asuras remain broken in the subterranean dark. The mortals offer their daily oblations without question.
Indra takes a leisurely sip of wine, looking out over the limitless expanse of his celestial capital.
INDRA
Peace is not given, Matali. It is seized. The universe recognizes strength. We are the gods because we conquered.
Indra casually raises his right hand. Between his knuckles, a sharp crack of BLUE LIGHTNING flickers—the VAJRA. He points it playfully toward a distant white cloud.
CRACK!
The lightning strikes the cloud. A shower of iridescent pearls cascades onto the laughing courtiers below. They scramble to gather the gems, chanting Indra's name in ecstatic worship.
Indra laughs—a sound devoid of reverence. Pure ego.
INDRA (CONT'D)
Time is a wheel, they say. But they forget: the wheel turns beneath the feet of my elephant!
Matali looks ahead down the avenue. His eyes narrow. His posture stiffens.
MATALI
My Lord... look.
Indra doesn't look down. He refills his goblet.
INDRA
What is it? Another Gandharva seeking a boon?
MATALI
No, My Lord. A wanderer.
Through the shimmering heat-haze at the end of the promenade, a solitary, dust-covered silhouette walks against the festive tide.
EXT. CROSSROADS OF THE HEAVENS - DAY#2#
The joyous celestial music cuts out abruptly. An uncanny, heavy silence smothers the avenue.
Standing in the center of the road, blocking the colossal bulk of Airavata, is—
SAGE DURVASA.
Matted locks like coiled black serpents. Skin smeared with grey funeral ash. Deep, hollow eyes that burn with the fierce, unshielded fire of Lord Shiva's Rudra energy. He wears a ragged deerskin across his sinewy shoulders.
In his calloused hands, he cradles a celestial GARLAND OF PARIJATA FLOWERS.
The garland does not wither; it pulses with a warm, golden, breathing luminescence. Celestial bees hover over it in ecstatic trance. It is the raw, living embodiment of SRI—Divine Fortune and Auspiciousness.
Airavata comes to a halt. His massive ears flap once.
Indra looks down from the howdah, mildly annoyed at the interruption.
INDRA
(condescending)
Brahmarshi Durvasa. To what does heaven owe the honor of your austere presence?
Durvasa takes a step forward. His voice is deep, gravelly, vibrating through the marble bedrock.
DURVASA
I bring no petition, Purandara. From the hands of the Divine Mother Herself, I received this garland of Sri. It is the essence of all fortune, all righteousness, all sovereignty. I bestow it upon thee, King of gods, as heaven’s sacred crown.
Durvasa holds the radiant garland upward.
Indra glances at it casually. Without descending from his howdah, without even setting down his wine goblet, he leans over the side and catches the garland with his LEFT hand.
INDRA
A fragrant gift, Rishi. Its perfume is indeed sweet.
Indra sniffs it once. He finds the scent overpowering. With a careless flick of his wrist—
HE TOSSES THE SACRED GARLAND ONTO THE FOREHEAD OF AIRAVATA.
Durvasa’s eyes widen. A flicker of disbelief.
ON AIRAVATA—
The celestial bees swarming the flowers begin buzzing aggressively around the elephant’s sensitive eyes.
Airavata snorts in irritation.
The colossal trunk curls upward.
THE TRUNK RIPS THE GARLAND FROM HIS FOREHEAD.
FLINGS IT DOWN ONTO THE DUSTY ROAD.
AND WITH A HEAVY, THUDDING STEP—
AIRAVATA’S MASSIVE GREY FOOT STOMPS DOWN DIRECTLY ONTO THE GARLAND.
CRUNCH.
Petals burst. Golden divine light splatters like crushed fruit and extinguishes in the dirt.
TOTAL SILENCE.
The courtiers freeze. Color drains from Matali’s face.
Durvasa stares at the muddy pulp beneath the elephant’s foot.
His staff begins to shake. His fingernails dig into his wooden rosary until it snaps.
When he raises his head, his eyes are no longer human—they are TWO WELLS OF LIVING, WHITE-HOT FLAME.
DURVASA
(voice cracking like thunder across the cosmos)
THOU FOOL!
A shockwave of sheer ascetic heat rips across the promenade. The rainbow arches overhead shudder.
Indra nearly drops his goblet. For the first time, fear enters his eyes.
INDRA
(stammering)
Holy one... wait! It was the beast—it was Airavata’s instinct, not my design—
DURVASA
THE BEAST OBEYS THE PRIDE OF ITS MASTER!
Thou hast sat upon thy golden throne so long thou hast forgotten that sovereignty is not thy property—it is a loan granted by GRACE!
And thou hast cast Grace into the dirt to be trampled underfoot!
Durvasa slams his staff onto the ground. A web of black cracks races through the gold pavement.
DURVASA (CONT'D)
HEAR MY WORD, LORD OF HEAVEN! Since thou hast treated Sri as worthless weed, SRI SHALL ABANDON THEE! Thy realms shall wither! Thy radiance shall turn to ash! The Three Worlds shall rot beneath a sky stripped of fortune and courage!
NISSHRIKA BHAVATU! THOU SHALT BE EMPTY!
Durvasa turns on his heel. His silhouette vanishes in a vortex of black ash.
IN THE AIR ABOVE AMARAVATI—
A sickening, low groaning sound.
The golden sky suddenly pales. The blossoms on every celestial tree wilt, turning black and brittle.
Indra looks down at his own hand.
The divine golden light radiating from his skin flickers... and DIES.
EXT. GATES OF AMARAVATI - NIGHT#3#
A terrifying, unnatural darkness has choked the heavens.
The celestial spires, once crystalline and luminous, are now stained with cold grey grime. Dead leaves blow across shattered parapets.
ON THE RAMPARTS—
DEVA SENTRIES lean against their spears. Their skin is sallow, their knuckles trembling. The divine armor hangs loose on their emaciated frames.
BELOW, ON THE CELESTIAL PLAIN—
BOOM.
BOOM.
A rhythmic, ground-splitting percussion. Drums made from the hide of planetary beasts.
Through the black fog marches an army of TITANS.
THE ASURAS. Hundreds of thousands. Towering silhouettes in jagged obsidian armor, horned war helms, carrying massive spiked clubs and serrated cleavers.
Leading the phalanx:
ASURA EMPEROR BALI.
Eight feet of raw, disciplined power. Clad in black mail with gold inlay. His eyes burn with cold, calculated intelligence. In his hands, he carries an obsidian greatsword.
Beside him ride NAMUCHI, SAMBARA, and KALANEMI—ferocious warlords thirsty for blood.
NAMUCHI
(snarling)
Look at the ramparts, Emperor! The cowards have snuffed their own torches! Their halos are gone!
BALI
Their halos did not die. Their spirit rotted. Bring up the ram. Break their heaven.
CRASH!
A colossal iron ram, shaped like a horned dragon, smashes into the adamantite gates of Amaravati.
The gates splinter like dry pine.
INSIDE THE COURTYARD—
INDRA stands desperately in the center, flanked by AGNI (pale, his flames barely flickering) and VAYU (gasping for breath).
INDRA
(raising the Vajra with trembling arms)
Hold the gates! Stand your ground, sons of Aditi! By the thunderbolt—
Indra swings the Vajra.
A pathetic, sputtering blue spark crawls three feet into the air and DIES with a faint fizz.
Bali kicks the shattered gate doors inward. He steps into the courtyard alone, looking around with a sneer.
BALI
Is this the thunderbolt that split mountains, Purandara?
Indra lunges forward with a spear.
Bali doesn't even draw his sword. With one bare, gauntleted hand, he CATCHES the spearhead in mid-thrust.
SNAP.
Bali snaps the divine spear in half like a twig. With a casual backhand blow, he strikes Indra across the face.
Indra flies backward across the flagstones, spitting blood, his golden crown rolling through the dirt.
Asura warriors flood the square, dragging down the golden banners of heaven and replacing them with black-and-crimson standards.
Bali steps up to the fallen King of Heaven. He places the heel of his iron boot onto Indra’s chest, pressing down until the god wheezes.
BALI (CONT'D)
You ruled the worlds by stolen light, Indra. Now the light has starved. Amaravati belongs to the children of Diti. Crawl into the void before I decide your head makes a poor trophy.
Bali steps off him and strides toward the throne hall.
Agni and Vayu drag the battered, weeping Indra backward into the shadows of the void.
INT. SATYA LOKA - LOTUS PAVILION - DAY#4#
A boundless realm of pure, translucent crystalline light.
SEATED UPON A RADIANT WHITE LOTUS—
LORD BRAHMA. Four crowned, silver-bearded heads looking toward the four quarters of space. His eyes are vast, tranquil, observing the infinite geometry of the cosmos.
CRAWLING ACROSS THE PRISTINE FLOOR—
THE DEVAS.
A pathetic trail of beaten, bleeding beggars.
Indra’s armor is gone, replaced by torn rags tied with rope. Behind him, Agni, Surya, and Varuna crawl on their hands and knees, weeping.
Indra throws his forehead onto the crystalline floor before Brahma’s lotus petals.
INDRA
(sobbing, voice ragged)
Grandfather! Father of all beings! The Daityas walk the courts of Amaravati! The sacrificial altars on Earth are choked with ash! We are hunted through the dark like vermin!
Brahma slowly opens all eight of his eyes. His expression is neither surprise nor fury—only deep, ancient sorrow.
BRAHMA
I know the sound of thy footstep, Indra. It is always loud in triumph, and weeping in defeat.
INDRA
Forge us a new weapon, Lord! Unleash the Brahmashira! Burn Bali’s citadels into glass! Restore our throne!
Brahma sighs. A gentle breeze stirs the petals.
BRAHMA
Still thou criest for weapons. Still thou comprehendest nothing.
Tell me, Purandara—did Bali steal thy crown with arrows? Did the Asuras overcome thee by virtue of greater austerity?
Indra hesitates, lowering his eyes in shame.
BRAHMA (CONT'D)
Thou hast cast away Sri with thine own hands. When the soul abandons the body, can a sword make the corpse stand?
Thy pride emptied heaven before Bali ever raised his ram.
INDRA
(desperate, clutching Brahma’s lotus base)
Then are we doomed? Shall unrighteousness reign forever? Is there no mercy in the cosmos?
BRAHMA
Creation is never without mercy. But the medicine thou requirest cannot be forged by Brahma. I can create the vessel, but I cannot sustain the falling world.
Only He who sleeps upon the waters can mend a broken universe.
Brahma rises from his lotus throne. His towering, four-headed form radiates serene purpose.
BRAHMA (CONT'D)
Rise, broken gods. Wash the dust from your eyes. We go to the shores of the Ocean of Milk.
EXT. SHORE OF KSHEERA SAGARA (OCEAN OF MILK) - TWILIGHT#5#
An endless horizon of liquid pearl. The waters do not crash—they roll in luminous, silent waves of warm white foam.
The sky above is an infinite gradient of deep cosmic indigo and starlight.
ON THE SHORE—
Brahma and the ragged assembly of Devas stand knee-deep in the milky foam.
Their voices rise together, chanting the ancient resonant verses of the PURUSHA SUKTA:
CHORUS OF DEVAS
Sahasra-śīrṣā puruṣaḥ sahasrākṣaḥ sahasrapāt...
Sa bhūmiṁ viśvato vṛtvāty-atiṣṭhad-daśāṅgulam...
IN THE DISTANCE—
The milky surface begins to shimmer with golden luminescence.
OUT OF THE WATERS RISES—
LORD VISHNU.
Colossal, serene, dark blue as a monsoon cloud heavy with rain. Clad in brilliant golden silks (Pitambara). Four arms holding the CONCH, the DISCUS, the MACE, and the LOTUS.
His face possesses an absolute, timeless calm—a smile that carries the gentle irony of eternity.
Indra falls to his knees in the foam, gasping.
INDRA
Lord! Save us! Strike the Asuras down from behind their walls! Unleash Thy Discus!
Vishnu raises His slender right hand. The gesture is small, yet it instantly stills the wind.
His voice resonates not in the air, but directly inside the minds of all present.
LORD VISHNU
Strike them, Indra? With what army? Thy warriors cannot hold a spear.
He who fights with anger against a stronger foe leaps into the furnace of his own destruction.
INDRA
Then what can we do? Surrender?
LORD VISHNU
Thou shalt master the craft of the asymmetric truce. Thou shalt make peace with Bali.
Indra looks up in horror.
INDRA
Peace? With the butcher who stands upon my throne?
LORD VISHNU
A treaty is not a surrender, Indra; it is the shield of the patient.
Thou and the Asuras shalt unite to perform the greatest labor since the dawn of time: thou shalt churn this Ocean of Milk.
Brahma leans forward, his four faces alight with understanding.
BRAHMA
The ocean... the repository of primordial seeds.
LORD VISHNU
Within these white depths lie both Sri—the lost grace of the worlds—and Amrita, the nectar of undying life.
Thou dost not have the physical strength to stir this sea alone. Thou needest the titan sinews of the Asuras. Offer them equal share in the nectar.
INDRA
(panicking)
Equal share? If Bali drinks Amrita, his tyranny becomes immortal! We seal our own doom!
Vishnu steps lightly across the surface of the foam, coming to stand directly before Indra.
His dark, lotus eyes pierce Indra’s soul with infinite depth.
LORD VISHNU
Dost thou think the nectar will rise without labor? The sea will yield many things before the cup appears—some sweet, and some so bitter they will crack the teeth of the arrogant.
Let the Asuras pull. Let them sweat.
Commit thy heart to Dharma, Indra, and leave the distribution of the fruit to Me. No crown won by raw ego can survive the touch of Grace.
INT. ASURA FORTRESS OF BALI - THRONE ROOM - NIGHT#6#
A cavernous hall of jagged volcanic basalt. Torches roar with green and violet sulfur fire.
Lining the colossal hall:
THOUSANDS OF ASURA GUARDS, standing at attention with hooked halberds and barbed spears.
WALKING DOWN THE CENTER AISLE—
INDRA.
Alone. Completely unarmed. Wearing the plain, white linen robes of an envoy rather than royal celestial armor.
Asura soldiers spit on the stones inches from his feet. Indra keeps his eyes locked straight ahead, his jaw rigid, enduring the humiliation.
AT THE FAR END OF THE HALL—
EMPEROR BALI lounges upon an obsidian throne draped in black fur.
Beside him stands SHUKRACHARYA, the one-eyed, white-bearded Preceptor of the Asuras, leaning on a serpent-headed staff.
Indra stops ten paces before the dais. He bows—formal, shallow, but undeniable.
BALI
(amused, mocking)
Look upon this, my generals! The Lord of Lightning walks into the mouth of the furnace.
Hast thou brought a ransom, Purandara? Or dost thou come to beg for the crumbs of thy former capital?
INDRA
I bring neither gold nor tears, Emperor. I bring the only thing thy swords cannot forge: immortality.
The hall falls silent. The Asura generals glance at one another.
Shukracharya’s single eye narrows.
SHUKRACHARYA
Beware, Bali. The gods speak of gifts only when their daggers are broken.
BALI
(leaning forward)
Speak, Indra. What madness is this?
INDRA
Thou hast conquered the Three Worlds, Bali. Yet look upon thy hair: time still greys it. Look upon thy soldiers: they still bleed. The cycle of death will one day drag even the Daitya empire into the grave.
Unless... we churn the Ocean of Milk.
BALI
The Milk Ocean? That boundless void?
INDRA
Within its depths sleeps AMRITA—the nectar of eternal life. But no single race has the power to stir an ocean of that mass.
Alone, we both decay. Together, we can churn the deep and claim undying life.
The Asura generals begin murmuring loudly.
"Amrita!" "To live forever!" "To conquer death itself!"
Shukracharya strikes his staff against the floor.
SHUKRACHARYA
A trap! It is Vishnu’s design! When the butter is churned, the thief always flees with the pot!
Bali holds up a massive armored gauntlet, silencing his guru. He stares down into Indra’s eyes, weighing the mortal stakes.
BALI
If we pull this sea, Indra, we pull it as equals. Fifty-fifty. Half the labor is ours—and half the nectar shall be poured into Asura chalices before thy lips ever touch a drop.
Swear it upon the cosmic order!
Indra holds his breath. He feels the weight of Vishnu's invisible hand on his shoulder.
INDRA
I swear it.
Bali smiles—ferocious, hungry, triumphant.
BALI
Then summon the legions! We march to the white sea!
EXT. SLOPES OF MOUNT MANDARA - CONTINENTAL HORIZON - DAY#7#
A mountain of sheer, mind-boggling scale.
MOUNT MANDARA. A golden-crested continental massif towering miles into the upper atmosphere, wrapped in primordial jungle and diamond-veined cliffs.
BELOW, AT THE BASE—
MILLIONS OF DEVAS AND ASURAS form an unbroken human-and-titan ring around the roots of the mountain.
They dig their fingers, crowbars, and divine halberds deep into the bedrock.
BALI
(roaring at his lines)
LIFT! PULL! HEAVE FOR IMMORTALITY!
INDRA
(straining, veins bulging)
HEAVE!
CRACK-BOOM!
A tectonic groan that registers across dimensions.
The planetary roots snap. The mountain TEARS FREE from the mantle of the earth.
Thousands of gods and titans stumble backward, then lock their arms underneath the titanic granite ledges, hoisting the continental mass onto their collective shoulders.
EXT. SCORCHED PLAINS - MIDDAY
The massive army shuffles forward, carrying the mountain like ants moving a boulder across a desert.
Sweat pours in rivers.
Sinews snap. Divine light flickers.
Fifty miles from the ocean—
A TITAN SLIPS.
The weight shifts violently to the left flank.
NAMUCHI
(screaming)
HOLD THE FLANK! IT’S TILTING!
CRACK.
A granite overhang shears off.
The sheer, crushing downward momentum of Mount Mandara becomes UNCONTROLLABLE.
INDRA
RUN! SCATTER!
BOOM!
The entire mountain drops like an asteroid.
A shockwave of pulverized dust and rock blooms thousands of feet into the air.
Tens of thousands of Devas and Asuras are crushed beneath millions of tons of falling granite cliffs.
The survivors lie coughing in the red dust, bleeding, paralyzed with terror.
Bali staggers to his feet, staring at the fallen mountain.
BALI
(spitting blood, voice hoarse)
We are fools... We tried to lift the axis of the world with mortal hands. It is over.
HIGH ABOVE—
A golden shadow blots out the sun.
FWOOSH!
A sonic displacement of air clears the dust in a single second.
THE COLOSSAL WINGS OF GARUDA.
The golden celestial eagle, spanning a league across, descends with majestic grace.
AND PERCHED UPON GARUDA’S SHOULDERS—
LORD VISHNU.
His blue skin radiates effortless, supreme majesty.
Vishnu looks down at the crushed armies. With a single glance of His merciful lotus eyes, a wave of golden nectar-light sweeps across the fallen.
THE CRUSHED WARRIORS RISE FROM THE DUST, WHOLE AND HEALED.
Then, Vishnu leans down from Garuda’s back.
With ONE BARE BLUE HAND, He catches the jagged golden summit of Mount Mandara.
HE LIFTS THE CONTINENTAL MOUNTAIN WITH ONE ARM.
Effortlessly, as if lifting a lotus stalk from a pond.
Garuda beats his wings. With a sonic roar, the eagle carries Vishnu and the mountain soaring over the coastline, depositing Mandara perfectly upright into the center of the Ocean of Milk.
SPLASH!
A wave of warm white foam washes up to the shores, greeting the astonished armies.
EXT. RIM OF KSHEERA SAGARA - MOUNT MANDARA BASE - DAY#8#
Mount Mandara stands upright in the center of the boundless white sea like a colossal golden pillar.
EMERGING FROM THE ABYSSAL SEA MIST—
VASUKI, King of the Nagas.
A serpent of cosmic proportions. Scales like polished emeralds; a hood that spans half the horizon, studded with blinding cosmic gems. His breath creates small whirlpools in the white sea.
Indra and Bali stand before him on the rocky shore, accompanied by Lord Vishnu.
LORD VISHNU
Great King of Serpents. The cosmos requires thy coils to serve as the living rope to churn these depths. Thy pain shall be great, but thy reward shall be immortal: a full vessel of Amrita.
Vasuki slowly lowers his gargantuan hood, dipping his crown into the milky foam in solemn acceptance.
VASUKI
Let my flesh be the rope of Dharma. Wrap me around the mountain.
Hundreds of thousands of warriors wade into the shallows, lifting Vasuki’s colossal scaly body and wrapping it THREE AND A HALF TIMES around the diamond ridges of Mount Mandara.
AS THE RANKS FORM—
Indra begins walking toward the HEAD of Vasuki, intending to take the front.
BALI instantly steps in front of him, followed by a dozen scowling Asura warlords with drawn daggers.
BALI
(snarling with fierce territorial pride)
Where dost thou think thou art walking, Indra?
INDRA
To the head of the serpent, to lead the pull.
BALI
Thou shalt lead nothing! We are the Daityas—the elder sons of creation! We conquered thy throne! Shall we hold the tail like common herdsmen wiping the dung of beasts?
Never! The post of honor belongs to the conquerors! We hold the head of the great serpent, or we tear this mountain down now!
Indra’s hand goes to his sword hilt. He looks back in anger.
ACROSS THE FOAM—
LORD VISHNU catches Indra’s gaze.
A faint, knowing smile plays upon Vishnu’s lips. Vishnu gives a subtle, almost imperceptible downward wave of His fingers—pointing toward the tail.
Indra understands. He exhales, relaxing his shoulders with theatrical reluctance.
INDRA
If the Emperor of the Asuras insists on holding the fangs... far be it from the defeated to quarrel. The tail is ours.
The Asuras roar in mockery and victorious laughter.
ASURA GENERALS
Cowards! Cling to the tail, little gods!
The Asuras take their positions, their gauntleted hands wrapping tightly around Vasuki’s throat, neck, and fangs.
On the opposite bank, the Devas grip the smooth, tapered tail.
Vasuki’s massive golden eyes blink. He breathes heavily.
VASUKI
(whispering under his breath)
They grip the serpent’s throat... and think they hold glory...
EXT. THE PRIMORDIAL OCEAN OF MILK - DAY#9#
A boundless expanse of white sea.
The two titanic armies stand braced on opposing shores, digging their boots into the sand, gripping the living cable of Vasuki.
A single horn sounds across the heavens.
BALI
(screaming with beastly fury)
HAUL!
INDRA
PULL!
THE FIRST TITANIC TUG.
Millions of arms flex in unison.
Vasuki groans, his scales grinding violently against the granite ridges of Mount Mandara.
THE MOUNTAIN ROTATES.
SKRRRR-BOOM!
A five-thousand-foot wall of boiling white milk erupts into the atmosphere. The ocean begins to churn.
Whirlpools miles wide tear through the surface.
Tectonic tremors ripple across the seabed.
AT THE HEAD OF THE SERPENT—
Vasuki’s eyes roll back in sheer agony.
From his parted jaws and flaring nostrils burst dense, suffocating clouds of BLACK SMOKE and ROARING FLAMES of scorching venom.
The searing heat blasts directly into the front ranks of the Asuras!
Armor plates glow red-hot. Asura warriors scream, their beards singed, skin blistered, gasping for air in the toxic sulfur cloud!
ASURA LIEUTENANT
(coughing violently, skin peeling)
The serpent breathes fire! We are burning!
BALI
(eyes watering, roaring through the smoke)
HOLD YOUR GROUND! DO NOT RELEASE THE HEAD!
AT THE TAIL OF THE SERPENT—
The Devas pull in smooth, synchronized rhythm.
Vasuki’s tapered tail whips through the air like a colossal fan, stirring up cool, perfumed ocean mists and celestial sea-breezes that revitalize Indra and his men.
Indra smiles grimly through the spray—Vishnu’s trick has worked.
ACCELERATION—
The mountain spins faster and faster, like a planetary drill bit boring into the ocean.
The milk turns to frothing, boiling cream.
AND THEN—
A sickening, subterranean THUD.
The ocean floor beneath Mount Mandara begins to disintegrate.
There is no bedrock. Only boundless primordial silt and infinite void.
Under the crushing downward torque of its own colossal rotation, MOUNT MANDARA BEGINS TO SLINK DOWNWARD.
INDRA
(shouting in panic)
The rope is slack! It’s slipping!
The mountain groans. It drops five hundred feet into the water.
Then a thousand feet.
Then the golden peak vanishes beneath the boiling white waves, swallowed whole by the abyssal deep.
THE CHURNING STOPS DEAD.
Silence falls. The water turns into a terrifying, whirlpool-scarred graveyard.
Without the mountain to serve as the axis of creation, the planetary plates begin to tilt. Cracks race across the sky.
BALI
(staggering back, covered in soot)
There is no bottom... The ocean is a bottomless grave. The mountain is gone!
INDRA
(falling to his knees)
The universe is falling inward! We have broken the world!
EXT. THE DEEP ABYSS BENEATH THE OCEAN OF MILK - CONTINUOUS#10#
Below the frothing white waves lies an infinite, silent abyss of luminous liquid ether.
Plunging downward like a falling meteor:
THE CRUSHING MASS OF MOUNT MANDARA.
Its jagged basalt root tears through the underwater currents, tumbling into the bottomless void.
OUT OF THE BLUE-BLACK DEEP—
A radiant sphere of golden divine light expands.
LORD VISHNU dissolves His four-armed form into the water.
FROM THAT DISSOLUTION MANIFESTS—
KURMA AVATAR.
A cosmic entity of unimaginable scale.
A titanic divine TORTOISE, a hundred thousand yojanas across.
His carapace is composed of indestructible cosmic basalt, veined with glowing diamond striations and ancient planetary runes. His ancient, serene eyes reflect the birth of galaxies.
Kurma glides smoothly upward through the milky currents, positioning His vast, rounded shell directly beneath the falling mountain.
THE COLLISION—
THUDDDDDDDDDDDDD.
A deep-frequency sub-bass shockwave reverberates through the foundations of creation.
The jagged, razor-sharp base of Mount Mandara strikes the dead center of Kurma’s shell.
THE MOUNTAIN COMES TO AN INSTANT, PERFECT HALT.
Kurma does not sink. He does not yield an inch.
The mountain sits upon the divine shell like an axle locked into a diamond hub.
Kurma blinks slowly. The unbearable weight of the continental massif feels like nothing more than a speck of dust upon His back.
ON THE OCEAN SURFACE—
Gods and Asuras stare in stunned awe as the golden summit of Mount Mandara EXPLODES BACK OUT OF THE WATER!
It rises higher, firmer, more unyielding than before.
ABOVE THE MOUNTAIN—
A golden, four-armed manifestation of LORD VISHNU materializes atop the highest peak, pressing His index finger onto the summit to keep it balanced from above.
SIMULTANEOUSLY—
Invisible streams of blue light flow from the ocean into the chests of the exhausted Devas, filling their lungs with renewed divine stamina.
Another stream enters the Asuras, keeping their colossal titan pride focused on the labor.
Another stream enters the spine of Vasuki, numbing the agony of his stretched vertebrae.
The Voice of Vishnu thunders across the cosmos:
VOICE OF VISHNU
THE AXIS IS HELD! PULL, CHILDREN OF CREATION! PULL WITH THE POWER OF DHARMA!
Bali lets out a war cry that shatters the sky.
Indra roars.
THE ARMIES HEAVE!
The mountain spins back to life with ten times its former velocity, its base grinding against Kurma’s shell with the sound of a roaring cosmic millstone.
Under the water, Kurma’s eyes close in blissful peace—the friction of the spinning mountain feels like a soothing scratching against His shell.
EXT. THE BOILING OCEAN OF MILK - CONTINUOUS#11#
The churning has reached blinding, supersonic speed.
The white waters are violently whipping into boiling froth. Lightning bolts of raw kinetic energy arc between the mountain and the clouds.
AND THEN—
A sickening, oily, subterranean GROAN.
Deep below, the friction between the mountain, the ocean depths, and Vasuki’s tortured internal organs ruptures the primordial under-veins of reality.
BURSTING THROUGH THE SURFACE—
A jet of thick, viscous, BLACK-BLUE SLUDGE.
It does not mix with the water. It combusts into a towering wall of corrosive, violet-black smoke.
HALAHALA. THE PRIMORDIAL POISON (KALAKUTA).
The smell hits first: the stench of charred bone, sulfur, rotting flesh, and cosmic decay.
The fumes expand across the horizon with terrifying speed.
ON THE SHORES—
A flock of celestial swans flying high in the stratosphere enters the fringe of the smoke.
Their feathers instantly dissolve. Their charred skeletons plummet into the surf.
IN THE ASURA RANKS—
The vanguard of Danava warriors drops their grip on Vasuki.
They claw at their own throats, screaming.
Their bronze armor turns green and melts against their flesh. They vomit black bile, their eyes turning milky blind.
NAMUCHI
(screaming, stumbling backward)
MY FLESH IS ON FIRE! IT’S EATING MY BONES!
BALI
(covering his mouth with an armored cloak)
FALL BACK! RETREAT TO THE CLIFFS!
IN THE DEVA RANKS—
Indra, Agni, and Vayu drop to their knees.
The air burns their lungs like boiling acid.
The milky sea itself begins to curdle into toxic, smoking black pitch.
INDRA
(coughing blood, looking skyward)
Lord Narayana! Where is the nectar? You promised us life—this is the extinction of the cosmos!
LORD BRAHMA’S voice thunders from the edge of the sky, vibrating with cosmic dread:
BRAHMA
This is Kalakuta! The accumulated poison of all material ambition!
No weapon can destroy it! No mantra can cleanse it! If it touches the soil of the mortal realm, every living seed shall burn into nonexistence!
INDRA
Then who can stop it? Can Vishnu not slay it?
BRAHMA
Vishnu sustains the universe—if He drinks it, the world inside Him perishes!
There is only One who possesses nothing, desires nothing, and fears nothing!
FLEE TO KAILASH! PLEAD BEFORE MAHADEVA!
The armies break. Devas and Asuras, former conquerors and proud gods, drop their weapons and scramble across the burning sands, running for their lives as the black cloud of doom rolls across the sky behind them.
EXT. SLOPES OF MOUNT KAILASH / BOILING SHORE - DAY#12#
A terrifying contrast of realms:
The pure, glacial white peaks of Mount Kailash are being devoured by swirling, violet-black fingers of toxic Halahala smoke.
SEATED IN PADMASANA UPON A TIGER SKIN—
LORD SHIVA (Mahadeva).
Naked to the waist, covered in pale sacred ash (Bhasma). Matted hair crowned with the crescent moon. A cobra coiled peacefully around His neck. His eyes are half-closed in unmoving, timeless meditation.
BESIDE HIM SITS—
GODDESS PARVATI. Regal, serene, wrapped in deep ochre silk, Her golden gaze watching the screaming sky.
CRAWLING UP THE FROZEN SLOPES—
Indra, Bali, Brahma, and the burned survivors of the cosmic armies collapse onto their faces before the ascetic lord.
BRAHMA
O Mahadeva! Lord of Yogis! The ocean has yielded its darkest secret! The poison of desire consumes the worlds! If Thou dost not drink the fire, creation perishes this hour!
Shiva slowly opens His three eyes.
They hold no fear. No anger. Only an infinite, unfathomable well of compassion.
He looks at Parvati. A silent dialogue passes between the eternal consort and Her Lord.
Parvati nods slowly. Her eyes glisten with divine sorrow, but Her chin remains high.
PARVATI
Go, My Lord. Swallowing the sorrow of the world is the duty of the Father.
Shiva rises. He does not take a weapon. Barefoot, He strides down the mountain slopes, stepping straight into the boiling, smoking black surf of the ocean.
AT THE EDGE OF THE VENOM—
The pool of Halahala sizzles, sending sparks of corrosive acid into the air.
Shiva bends down.
HE CUPS HIS BARE PALMS INTO THE POOL OF DEATH.
The black-blue liquid fills His cupped hands, bubbling violently, trying to dissolve His flesh. Shiva’s hands do not flinch.
HE BRINGS HIS PALMS TO HIS LIPS.
AND DRINKS THE ENTIRE OCEAN OF POISON LIKE WATER.
HISSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSS.
A sound like water hitting white-hot iron.
As the lethal venom races down Shiva’s throat—
PARVATI APPEARS BESIDE HIM.
Her divine golden hand reaches out with lightning precision.
SHE GRIPS SHIVA’S THROAT TIGHTLY WITH HER FINGERS.
PARVATI (CONT'D)
Stop here, Kalakuta! By the power of the Mother, thou shalt not descend into the belly where the living worlds reside!
Shiva engages His internal ascetic stillness (Tapas).
The poison is trapped. It cannot go down into His stomach. It cannot spill back out of His mouth.
BEFORE THE ASTONISHED EYES OF THE HOSTS—
Shiva’s fair throat BURNS.
The skin darkens from pale gold into bruised midnight, then into deep, radiant, luminescent COBALT BLUE.
NEELAKANTHA. The Blue-Throated Savior.
Shiva exhales. A tiny wisp of harmless blue steam curls from His lips.
A few stray droplets slip through His fingers into the surf—snakes, scorpions, and toxic roots greedily lap them up, claiming their earthly venom.
The black cloud clears from the sky.
The sunlight breaks through, pure and golden.
Shiva smiles, serene, untouched. The Three Worlds fall to their knees in weeping reverence.
EXT. THE PURIFIED OCEAN OF MILK - DAY#13#
The waters of Ksheera Sagara have returned to immaculate, diamond-white radiance.
The sunlight bounces off crystal waves.
Devas and Asuras, their burns healed and lungs cleared by Shiva's grace, haul Vasuki’s coils with renewed, sacred frenzy.
Mount Mandara spins smoothly on Kurma’s shell, humming like a cosmic instrument.
AND THEN—THE HARVEST BEGINS.
THE FOAM HEAVES.
OUT OF THE WHITE SURF RISES—
KAMADHENU (SURABHI).
An immaculate, golden-horned celestial cow. Her eyes radiate the infinite nourishment of motherhood; milk streams from her udders, turning the surf into nectar.
The SAGES—Kashyapa, Vashistha, and Angiras—step forward with folded palms.
KASHYAPA
The mother of all offerings!
LORD VISHNU’S voice speaks from the golden summit:
LORD VISHNU (O.S.)
Take her, holy sages. Let her sustain the eternal fires of Yajna upon the earth.
THE SURF BOILS AGAIN.
With a thunderous whinny that sounds like lightning:
UCCHAIHSHRAVAS.
A pure white, seven-headed stallion, leaping over the cresting waves like a creature made of crystallized light.
EMPEROR BALI pushes through his ranks, his eyes blazing with imperial greed.
BALI
The stallion of supreme velocity! He belongs to the master of the worlds! The Asuras claim him!
Bali grabs the celestial reins, mounting the seven-headed beast with a triumphant roar.
ANOTHER RUMBLE—
Four massive, ivory tusks break the waves.
AIRAVATA, accompanied by his mate ABHRAMU, emerges from the depths, spraying water through his trunk like twin fountains.
Indra’s face lights up with tears of joy. He runs forward, throwing his arms around the colossal elephant’s trunk.
INDRA
Airavata! My companion! Heaven’s crest has returned!
AND THEN—TOTAL, BLINDING RADIANCE.
From the dead center of the swirling deep, a single beam of ruby-violet light shoots thousands of miles into the sky.
THE KAUSTUBHA MANI.
A gem of unearthly, pure brilliance. It does not reflect external light—it radiates the pure, unblemished consciousness of reality itself.
The jewel hovers above the water, moving as if guided by an invisible will.
It glides past the reaching hands of Bali.
It glides past Indra.
IT SETTLES GENTLY UPON THE BROAD, DARK CHEST OF LORD VISHNU.
The Kaustubha locks into place directly over His heart, pulsing in perfect rhythm with the Sustainer’s breath.
BALI
(lowering his reaching hand, muttering)
A gem that chooses its own master...
EXT. THE PRIMORDIAL OCEAN OF MILK - CONTINUOUS#14#
The churning continues with rhythmic, hypnotic grace. The ocean yields its aesthetic and cultural treasures:
* THE PARIJATA TREE rises from the foam, its branches blooming with unfading, aromatic blossoms that fill the cosmos with divine scent (taken to Swarga).
* THE APSARAS—Rambha, Menaka, Tilottama—emerge laughing, their celestial footfalls chiming like temple bells as they take flight.
* THE KALPAVRIKSHA, the tree of desires.
* THE SHARNGA BOW AND PANCHAJANYA CONCH, which soar like eagles straight into the hands of Lord Vishnu.
AND OUT OF THE FOAM RISES—
CHANDRA (SOMA).
The luminous, cool crescent moon, glowing with icy silver light.
Seeing the heat still radiating from the blue neck of Lord Shiva on Kailash, Chandra glides across the sky and rests gently within the matted locks of Mahadeva, casting a soothing, permanent silver halo that cools the burning venom.
THEN—THE WATERS ROLL HEAVILY.
A rich, intoxicating aroma of fermented honey and spiced flowers fills the air.
BURSTING THROUGH THE FOAM—
VARUNI (SURA).
The Goddess of Wine and Intoxication.
Her dark hair is wild, draped in flowering vines; her eyes are half-closed, heavy with ecstatic inebriation. In Her hands She carries overflowing golden flagons of crimson wine.
The Devas look upon Her and hesitate, keeping their minds focused on the ultimate goal: Amrita.
THE ASURAS, HOWEVER—
A collective roar of animal hunger erupts from the Danava ranks.
Generals and foot soldiers break formation, dropping Vasuki’s neck, rushing toward the surf with outstretched chalices.
ASURA WARRIOR
(laughing wildly)
Wine from the deep! The gods can keep their dry trees! Give us the liquid fire!
Varuni laughs—a rich, mocking, seductive sound—and pours the golden-red wine into their waiting cups.
The Asura warriors drink like dying men in a desert, wine spilling over their beards, their eyes turning bloodshot with drunken arrogance.
Bali watches his men, frowning slightly, but does not stop them.
BALI
Let them drink. We have pulled like oxen; we deserve the feast of kings.
ON THE OPPOSING SHORE—
Indra watches the drunken revelry with cold, strategic satisfaction.
INDRA
(whispering to Agni)
Look at them. Intoxicated before the battle is even won. They swallow their own vigilance.
EXT. THE PRIMORDIAL OCEAN OF MILK - CENTER - DAY#15#
WITHOUT WARNING—
The violent rotation of Mount Mandara slows, then CEASES ENTIRELY.
The serpent Vasuki rests his weary emerald hood upon the waves.
A sudden, profound, sacred stillness settles over the Three Worlds.
The roaring wind dies. The frothing white surf subsides, transforming into an unblemished, mirror-like sheet of LIQUID MOLTEN GOLD.
IN THE DEAD CENTER OF THE OCEAN—
Ripples of pure light expand outward.
From the golden depths rises a COLOSSAL, THOUSAND-PETALED LOTUS.
The pink petals unfold one by one with slow, hypnotic, majestic grace.
AND STANDING UPON THE LOTUS PERICARP—
GODDESS MAHALAKSHMI (SRI).
A vision that stops the breath of every living being in creation.
Her skin is burnished, radiant molten gold.
Her lotus-petal eyes hold the infinite, tender mercy of the Mother of the Universe, tempered by absolute, unbending sovereignty.
She is draped in shimmering crimson silks woven from dawn-light; Her golden waist-chain and anklets chime with the secret music of creation.
In Her hands, She holds two pink lotuses.
FROM HER PALMS—
An invisible shower of divine auspiciousness radiates outward in golden waves.
Across the universe:
* Dead, withered trees in heaven burst into vibrant green leaves and blossoms.
* The sallow, wrinkled skin of the Devas instantly fills with youthful golden radiance.
* Earthly fields, parched by drought, bloom with golden grain.
* Sacrificial fires on Earth leap into clear, sweet-smelling flames.
SRI HAS RETURNED.
THE FOUR DIGGAJAS—the cosmic guardian elephants of space—step onto the golden water with reverent, thundering grace.
They raise their massive trunks, holding golden ewers filled with crystal water from the celestial river Mandakini.
THE GAJA-LAKSHMI ABHISHEKA.
The elephants pour the sanctified streams over Mahalakshmi’s crown. The water cascades down Her dark hair, turning into sparkling pearls as it touches Her lotus pedestal.
Celestial Gandharvas begin to sing the sacred verses of the SRI SUKTAM.
Rain of unfading Parijata blossoms falls like a warm, fragrant blizzard.
EXT. RIM OF THE OCEAN OF MILK - CONTINUOUS#16#
The labor of the churning is instantly forgotten.
Amrita is forgotten. Vasuki is forgotten.
Every pair of eyes—god, titan, king, and sage—is fixed upon GODDESS MAHALAKSHMI with desperate, consuming lust.
To possess Her is to possess eternal sovereignty, fortune, and supreme victory.
THE COALITION DISSOLVES INTO CHAOS.
ON THE ASURA SHORE—
The Danava warlords push past Emperor Bali, their weapons clattering as they swagger into the golden shallows.
KALANEMI
(shouting, pounding his iron breastplate)
Look upon our empire, Goddess! We broke the gates of Amaravati! We offer Thee cities of black obsidian and mountains of gold plunder!
Thou art the prize of the conqueror!
SAMBARA
The Asuras shed the most blood for this churning! By right of blood and steel, She belongs to our house!
ON THE DEVA SHORE—
INDRA steps forward, adjusting his diamond crown, puffed up with celestial pride.
INDRA
Stand back, demons! She arose from the light—she belongs to Swarga!
Lady, I am Purandara, King of Heaven, wielder of the thunderbolt! Come with me to the golden throne of heaven! Thou shalt rule the Three Worlds by my side!
THE SAGES AND ASCETICS step forward, rattling their wooden prayer beads.
AN ASCETIC
Kings offer only dust! I offer Thee the merit of ten thousand years of unbending penance!
THE SCENE BECOMES A DEAFENING AUCTION OF EGOS.
Gods, demons, and sages shout their credentials, rattling their scimitars, waving their deeds of conquest, treating the Divine Mother of the Universe as a trophy to be won by brute force, status, or transactional barter.
UPON HER LOTUS—
Mahalakshmi does not speak.
Her lotus eyes gaze upon the screaming assembly with cool, piercing discernment.
She sees right through their polished armor:
They do not love Her.
They love what She can do for them.
They desire the crown of Fortune, not the heart of Grace.
EXT. THE SURFACE OF THE OCEAN OF MILK - CONTINUOUS#17#
The clamor of the suitors dies away as GODDESS MAHALAKSHMI moves.
She raises both hands.
From the heart of the golden lotus, She lifts the VANAMALA—an unfading, fragrant garland of deep blue lotuses, Parijata blossoms, and wild forest flowers.
Humming celestial bees circle the blossoms, drunk on its heavenly perfume.
MAHALAKSHMI STEPS DOWN OFF THE LOTUS.
Her bare, pink-soled feet touch the surface of the golden water.
The water does not break—it supports Her steps with gentle, golden ripples.
Holding the sacred garland between Her slender fingers, She begins Her SLOW, MAJESTIC WALK OF SOVEREIGNTY down the line of assembled suitors.
TOTAL, BREATHLESS SILENCE DESCENDS ON THE THREE WORLDS.
The only sound is the faint, musical chiming of Her golden anklets: chink... chink... chink...
SHE GLIDES BEFORE SAGE DURVASA AND THE RISHIS.
Durvasa stands proud, waiting for the garland.
Mahalakshmi’s eyes meet his.
LAKSHMI (V.O.)
(internal, echoing across space)
You possess vast ascetic heat, Brahmarshi... yet your anger is an untamed fire. You have tapas, but no unshakeable peace. Grace cannot dwell in a vessel cracked by rage.
She glides past him. Durvasa’s staff lowers; his proud eyes blink in shock.
SHE GLIDES BEFORE LORD BRAHMA.
Brahma smiles with grandfatherly affection, inclining his four heads.
LAKSHMI (V.O.) (CONT'D)
Venerable Grandfather... You are the seed-giver of forms, bound to the duty of cosmic progeny and detachment. You are the architect, not the companion of dynamic preservation.
She bows Her head respectfully and glides past.
SHE GLIDES BEFORE LORD SHIVA.
Mahadeva sits upon the shore, His blue throat gleaming, Parvati at His side. His eyes hold zero desire.
LAKSHMI (V.O.) (CONT'D)
O Mahadeva... You are supremely pure, beyond all need, complete in Your own stillness. You are already wedded to the burning ground and to the Mountain’s Daughter.
Shiva nods gently in silent blessing as She passes.
SHE GLIDES BEFORE INDRA.
Indra stands tall, chest puffed, offering the diamond sceptre of heaven.
LAKSHMI (V.O.) (CONT'D)
And thou, Purandara... Thou hast majesty, yet thy heart is fickle as desert wind. Intoxicated in victory, a coward in defeat. Thou seekest Me to cower behind My light, not to champion Dharma.
She walks past him without pausing.
Indra’s arms drop to his sides, his face turning pale with devastating humiliation.
SHE GLIDES BEFORE EMPEROR BALI AND THE ASURAS.
Bali stands with hand on his sword, jaw rigid, breathing heavily.
LAKSHMI (V.O.) (CONT'D)
And thou, Emperor of Titans. Thy courage is immense, thy generosity vast... yet thy soul is enslaved to raw ego. Thou seekest to possess Me as a conqueror possesses a province. But Grace is no man’s slave.
She walks past the Asuras.
Bali’s hand slips from his hilt. The Asura generals stand frozen in stunned disbelief as Her golden shadow leaves them behind.
She has rejected the entire power structure of the universe.
EXT. HORIZON OF THE OCEAN OF MILK - CONTINUOUS#18#
At the very edge of the vast assembly, standing solitary upon the golden waters, is—
LORD VISHNU.
He holds no weapon drawn. He wears no haughty smirk. He has offered no boast, made no demand, and taken not a single step toward Her.
He stands in majestic, effortless stillness (Atmarama—self-fulfilled).
The Kaustubha gem pulses upon His chest like a beating star; His yellow silk flutters in the fragrant breeze.
GODDESS MAHALAKSHMI approaches Him.
She comes to a halt two paces away.
She looks up into His dark, monsoon-cloud face.
He looks down into Her golden, lotus eyes.
In that single, timeless gaze, the separation of billions of years dissolves. A profound, warm light fills the entire universe.
Mahalakshmi smiles—a smile of homecoming, surrender, and supreme victory.
SHE RAISES BOTH HANDS.
She lifts the heavy, fragrant VANAMALA garland of blue lotuses and celestial blossoms.
Vishnu bows His dark, crowned head with tender reverence.
SHE PLACES THE GARLAND AROUND HIS NECK.
THE COSMIC UNION.
As the cool blossoms settle onto His broad chest, Lord Vishnu raises His right hand in the gesture of fearlessness (Abhaya Mudra).
With His left arm, He draws Her gently to His side.
HE PLACES HER ETERNALLY UPON HIS CHEST.
She dissolves into a radiant golden sigil over His heart: SHRIVATSA and HRIDAYAVASINI.
Where Vishnu stands, there Sri shall dwell forever.
A ROAR OF HEAVENLY CONCHES SHATTERS THE STRATOSPHERE!
The Gandharvas weep with joy; celestial drums thunder; rain of parijata blossoms covers the golden sea.
AND AS THEIR SPIRITS MERGE, THEIR SILENT VOW REVERBERATES ACROSS TIME:
LAKSHMI (V.O.)
(vibrating through the yugas)
Whenever the cosmic balance falters... whenever Thou descendest into mortal flesh to lift the burden of the earth... I shall precede Thee.
I shall take birth upon the mortal soil.
I shall endure the walls of mortal palaces and the threats of arrogant kings.
And when Thou comest to claim Me, I shall look across the assembly of false suitors, bypass them all, and place my heart into Thy hands alone.
EXT. THE PRIMORDIAL OCEAN OF MILK - AFTERNOON#19#
The celestial music of the wedding still echoes across the golden waves.
The waters churn one final time.

LORD DHANVANTARI.
Radiant with youth and primordial vitality. Clad in bright saffron silk, adorned with garlands of sacred medicinal herbs.

NAMUCHI

AMRITA IS OURS! WE DRINK THE LIFE OF THE WORLDS!

INDRA
Traitors! Thieves! Charge the camp!

LORD VISHNU
Sheath thy sword, Indra. Greed carries a swifter poison than the serpent’s tooth. Watch.

YOUNG DANAVA WARRIOR
Give us the urn! We stormed the Healer first! We drink first!

Back, dog! This belongs to the commanders!

EXT. ASURA ENCAMPMENT - SHORE OF MILK OCEAN - AFTERNOON#20#
The bloody civil war among the Asura warlords is in full frenzy.
Swords dripping with Daitya blood ring the golden Amrita urn resting in the sand.
Bali stands between two factions, his armor dented, trying to hold his generals back from tearing one another apart.
SUDDENLY—
A gentle, perfumed breeze scented with blue water lilies and sandalwood sweeps through the camp.
A faint, rhythmic chime of silver waist-bells (mekhala) and golden anklets: chink... chime... chink...
The warlords freeze.
Swords lower. Heavy breathing slows.
STEPPING INTO THE CAMP—
MOHINI MURTI.
The feminine incarnation of LORD VISHNU.
An embodiment of enchantment (Maya) so devastating, so breathtakingly beautiful that reality seems to bend around Her.
She glides like a royal swan across the blood-splattered sand.
Draped in translucent, multicolored silks that flutter in the breeze; eyes dark and deep as midnight lotus pools; lips curved into an amused, tantalizing smile that promises the universe while conceding nothing.
The Asura generals stumble backward, their faces flushing red like chastised schoolboys.
Namuchi hurriedly hides his bloody scimitar behind his back.
Bali stands completely captivated, his sword lowering to his side.
MOHINI
(voice like liquid honey, teasing gently)
Are these the mighty conquerors of heaven?
The great sons of Diti, masters of three realms... squabbling like hungry street mongrels over an earthen bowl?
How tragic that so much strength possesses so little grace.
She tilts Her head, Her eyes sparkling with playful mockery.
Bali steps forward, completely spellbound.
He picks up the heavy, golden AMRITA KUMBHA with both trembling hands and extends it outward toward Her.
BALI
O maiden of peerless beauty! Who art Thou?
We cannot trust the gods, and we cannot trust our own brothers. But we surrender our prize, our honor, and our souls to Thee!
Take this urn! Distribute it fairly among us all with Thine own fair hands!
Mohini lets out an enchanting, musical laugh—mocking and dangerous.
MOHINI
Trust a woman? Have you not heard the counsel of the wise? The sages say a woman’s mind is fickle as the ocean wind.
I might give it all to the Devas! I might pour it into the sand! Why would mighty warlords surrender their immortality to a maiden they have never known?
Her hesitation only inflames their desperate infatuation.
The Asura generals drop to their knees around Her.
ASURA GENERALS
We swear by our blood! Whatever Thy fair hands pour, we shall accept without question!
Only distribute the nectar, O enchanting one!
Mohini smiles—a brilliant, predatory flash of divine wit.
She extends Her slender, golden fingers and takes the urn by its handle.
MOHINI
Then go, warriors. Wash the blood from your hands. Sit in peace. The feast of immortality is prepared.
EXT. THE BANQUET PAVILION OF THE OCEAN - SUNSET#21#
A blood-red and gold sunset blazes across the white waves.
MOHINI has arranged the hosts in TWO PARALLEL ROWS:
* On the LEFT: THE ASURAS, seated cross-legged, staring at Mohini with glazed, enchanted smiles, drunk on Her beauty and teasing glances.

SPLASH... SPLASH... SPLASH...
The Devas drink, their bodies blazing with immortal golden vigor.
ACROSS THE AISLE—
The Asuras sit in rapturous stupor. Whenever an Asura reaches out his cup, Mohini turns with a teasing laugh, flashes a dazzling smile, touches his chin, and whispers:

ONE ASURA IS NOT ENCHANTED.
SVARBHANU.
A dark, cunning titan sorcerer. His eyes burn with cold calculation.

Mohini glides to the end of the row.
She approaches Svarbhanu. She tilts the golden ladle.
A SINGLE DROP OF AMRITA FALLS INTO SVARBHANU’S CUPPED HANDS.
Svarbhanu brings his hands to his mouth with trembling speed.
The liquid touches his lips.

SURYA looks sideways. He notices the shadow cast by the warrior.
CHANDRA sees the serpentine fangs beneath the divine illusion.
SURYA & CHANDRA
(shouting in horror)
IMPOSTOR! IT IS SVARBHANU!
MOHINI DROPS THE PLAYFUL FACADE IN A FRACTION OF A SECOND.
Her feminine eyes flash with the blinding, apocalyptic wrath of LORD VISHNU.
Her right hand extends.
FWOOSH!
THE SUDARSHANA CHAKRA.
A wheel of blinding, thousand-toothed solar fire materializes spinning above Her palm.
SHE HURLS THE DISCUS!
A laser of living flame streaks across the hall.
CLEAVE.
The Discus cleanly slices through Svarbhanu’s neck!
Blood sprays across the marble.
The neck is severed—BUT THE NECTAR HAD ALREADY TOUCHED HIS THROAT!
BECAUSE HE DRANK AMRITA, NEITHER HALF CAN DIE!
THE SEVERED HEAD—
Rises into the air, its mouth opening in an eternal, roaring scream:
RAHU.
THE SERPENT-LIKE TORSO—
Thrashing in the sand, sprouts coils and wings:
KETU.
The Sudarshana Chakra hurls both halves into the celestial sphere, where they are cast into the dark void—forever destined to pursue Surya and Chandra across the sky as the cosmic eclipses.
The urn is empty.
Mohini turns toward the Asura row, the divine Discus spinning quietly behind Her hand.
The enchantment breaks like shattered glass.
BALI
(leaping to his feet, eyes wide)
WE HAVE BEEN TRICKED!
EXT. THE CELESTIAL BATTLEFIELD OF THE SEA / AMARAVATI - NIGHT / DAWN#22#
The twilight shatters into war.
With roars of enraged humiliation, the titan armies draw their massive iron cleavers and charge across the golden shallows.
ASURA WARLORDS
SLAUGHTER THEM! BREAK HEAVEN TO DUST!
BUT THE DEVAS ARE NO LONGER WEAK.
Filled with the undying, radiant vigor of AMRITA, the bodies of the gods blaze like miniature suns!
Their withered limbs have returned to godlike perfection.
INDRA
(roaring atop Airavata, raising the Vajra)
FOR DHARMA! FOR THE SOVEREIGNTY OF LIGHT!
KABOOOOM!
The Vajra unleashes a cataclysmic blast of white lightning that cleaves the Asura vanguard in two!
Agni unleashes a river of sacred celestial fire.
Vayu commands a hurricane that sweeps titan chariots into the upper atmosphere.
In a ferocious, sweeping planetary engagement, the revitalized Deva legions, backed by Lord Vishnu’s invincible aura, overwhelm the Daitya armies, driving Emperor Bali and the survivors back down into the abyssal depths of Patala.
DISSOLVE TO:
INT. THRONE ROOM OF AMARAVATI - DAWN
The golden banners of heaven are restored to the crystalline spires.
The sunlight washes through the hall, pure and eternal.
INDRA stands before his golden throne.
Beside the throne stand LORD VISHNU and GODDESS MAHALAKSHMI, radiant in unified celestial majesty.
Indra does not mount the throne with a boast.
He removes his diamond crown with both hands.
He kneels before Vishnu and Lakshmi, placing his crown at their divine lotus feet.
INDRA
Forgive my ancient arrogance, Lord of all beings. Mother of the Worlds.
Now I know: Amaravati is not held by the thunderbolt, but by Thy Grace alone. Let me rule only as a servant of Dharma.
Vishnu smiles, raising His hand in blessing.
LORD VISHNU
Rule with justice, Indra, for the wheel of time never sleeps. When the cycle turns again, and pride once more poisons the earth... the churning shall begin anew.
Vishnu and Lakshmi turn toward the infinite horizon of space, their divine silhouettes merging into the eternal radiance of VAIKUNTHA.
FADE OUT.
THE END
