# 🎬 SCREENPLAY SCENE 15: THE GOLDEN VORTEX AND GAJA-LAKSHMI ABHISHEKA

**EXT. THE PRIMORDIAL OCEAN OF MILK - CENTER - DAY**

WITHOUT WARNING—

The violent rotation of Mount Mandara slows, then CEASES ENTIRELY. 
The serpent Vasuki rests his weary emerald hood upon the waves.

A sudden, profound, sacred stillness settles over the Three Worlds. 
The roaring wind dies. The frothing white surf subsides, transforming into an unblemished, mirror-like sheet of LIQUID MOLTEN GOLD.

IN THE DEAD CENTER OF THE OCEAN—

Ripples of pure light expand outward. 
From the golden depths rises a COLOSSAL, THOUSAND-PETALED LOTUS. 
The pink petals unfold one by one with slow, hypnotic, majestic grace.

AND STANDING UPON THE LOTUS PERICARP—

GODDESS MAHALAKSHMI (SRI).

A vision that stops the breath of every living being in creation.
Her skin is burnished, radiant molten gold. 
Her lotus-petal eyes hold the infinite, tender mercy of the Mother of the Universe, tempered by absolute, unbending sovereignty. 
She is draped in shimmering crimson silks woven from dawn-light; Her golden waist-chain and anklets chime with the secret music of creation. 
In Her hands, She holds two pink lotuses.

FROM HER PALMS—

An invisible shower of divine auspiciousness radiates outward in golden waves.
Across the universe:
* Dead, withered trees in heaven burst into vibrant green leaves and blossoms.
* The sallow, wrinkled skin of the Devas instantly fills with youthful golden radiance.
* Earthly fields, parched by drought, bloom with golden grain.
* Sacrificial fires on Earth leap into clear, sweet-smelling flames.

SRI HAS RETURNED.

THE FOUR DIGGAJAS—the cosmic guardian elephants of space—step onto the golden water with reverent, thundering grace. 
They raise their massive trunks, holding golden ewers filled with crystal water from the celestial river Mandakini.

THE GAJA-LAKSHMI ABHISHEKA.
The elephants pour the sanctified streams over Mahalakshmi’s crown. The water cascades down Her dark hair, turning into sparkling pearls as it touches Her lotus pedestal.

Celestial Gandharvas begin to sing the sacred verses of the SRI SUKTAM. 
Rain of unfading Parijata blossoms falls like a warm, fragrant blizzard.
