# 🎬 SCREENPLAY SCENE 09: THE CHURNING COMMENCES AND MANDARA SINKS

**EXT. THE PRIMORDIAL OCEAN OF MILK - DAY**

A boundless expanse of white sea. 
The two titanic armies stand braced on opposing shores, digging their boots into the sand, gripping the living cable of Vasuki.

A single horn sounds across the heavens.

                    BALI
          (screaming with beastly fury)
     HAUL!

                    INDRA
     PULL!

THE FIRST TITANIC TUG.
Millions of arms flex in unison. 
Vasuki groans, his scales grinding violently against the granite ridges of Mount Mandara.

*THE MOUNTAIN ROTATES.*

*SKRRRR-BOOM!*

A five-thousand-foot wall of boiling white milk erupts into the atmosphere. The ocean begins to churn.
Whirlpools miles wide tear through the surface. 
Tectonic tremors ripple across the seabed.

AT THE HEAD OF THE SERPENT—

Vasuki’s eyes roll back in sheer agony. 
From his parted jaws and flaring nostrils burst dense, suffocating clouds of BLACK SMOKE and ROARING FLAMES of scorching venom.
The searing heat blasts directly into the front ranks of the Asuras!
Armor plates glow red-hot. Asura warriors scream, their beards singed, skin blistered, gasping for air in the toxic sulfur cloud!

                    ASURA LIEUTENANT
          (coughing violently, skin peeling)
     The serpent breathes fire! We are burning!

                    BALI
          (eyes watering, roaring through the smoke)
     HOLD YOUR GROUND! DO NOT RELEASE THE HEAD!

AT THE TAIL OF THE SERPENT—

The Devas pull in smooth, synchronized rhythm. 
Vasuki’s tapered tail whips through the air like a colossal fan, stirring up cool, perfumed ocean mists and celestial sea-breezes that revitalize Indra and his men. 
Indra smiles grimly through the spray—Vishnu’s trick has worked.

ACCELERATION—

The mountain spins faster and faster, like a planetary drill bit boring into the ocean. 
The milk turns to frothing, boiling cream.

AND THEN—

A sickening, subterranean *THUD*.

The ocean floor beneath Mount Mandara begins to disintegrate. 
There is no bedrock. Only boundless primordial silt and infinite void.

Under the crushing downward torque of its own colossal rotation, MOUNT MANDARA BEGINS TO SLINK DOWNWARD.

                    INDRA
          (shouting in panic)
     The rope is slack! It’s slipping!

The mountain groans. It drops five hundred feet into the water. 
Then a thousand feet. 
Then the golden peak vanishes beneath the boiling white waves, swallowed whole by the abyssal deep.

THE CHURNING STOPS DEAD.

Silence falls. The water turns into a terrifying, whirlpool-scarred graveyard. 
Without the mountain to serve as the axis of creation, the planetary plates begin to tilt. Cracks race across the sky.

                    BALI
          (staggering back, covered in soot)
     There is no bottom... The ocean is a bottomless
     grave. The mountain is gone!

                    INDRA
          (falling to his knees)
     The universe is falling inward! We have broken
     the world!
