# 🎬 SCREENPLAY SCENE 18: THE COSMIC SWAYAMVARA AND ETERNAL VOW

**EXT. HORIZON OF THE OCEAN OF MILK - CONTINUOUS**

At the very edge of the vast assembly, standing solitary upon the golden waters, is—

LORD VISHNU.

He holds no weapon drawn. He wears no haughty smirk. He has offered no boast, made no demand, and taken not a single step toward Her. 
He stands in majestic, effortless stillness (*Atmarama*—self-fulfilled). 
The Kaustubha gem pulses upon His chest like a beating star; His yellow silk flutters in the fragrant breeze. 

GODDESS MAHALAKSHMI approaches Him. 

She comes to a halt two paces away. 
She looks up into His dark, monsoon-cloud face. 
He looks down into Her golden, lotus eyes.

In that single, timeless gaze, the separation of billions of years dissolves. A profound, warm light fills the entire universe.

Mahalakshmi smiles—a smile of homecoming, surrender, and supreme victory.

SHE RAISES BOTH HANDS.

She lifts the heavy, fragrant **VANAMALA** garland of blue lotuses and celestial blossoms. 

Vishnu bows His dark, crowned head with tender reverence.

SHE PLACES THE GARLAND AROUND HIS NECK.

*THE COSMIC UNION.*

As the cool blossoms settle onto His broad chest, Lord Vishnu raises His right hand in the gesture of fearlessness (*Abhaya Mudra*). 
With His left arm, He draws Her gently to His side.

HE PLACES HER ETERNALLY UPON HIS CHEST. 

She dissolves into a radiant golden sigil over His heart: **SHRIVATSA** and **HRIDAYAVASINI**. 
Where Vishnu stands, there Sri shall dwell forever.

A ROAR OF HEAVENLY CONCHES SHATTERS THE STRATOSPHERE!
The Gandharvas weep with joy; celestial drums thunder; rain of parijata blossoms covers the golden sea.

AND AS THEIR SPIRITS MERGE, THEIR SILENT VOW REVERBERATES ACROSS TIME:

                    LAKSHMI (V.O.)
          (vibrating through the yugas)
     Whenever the cosmic balance falters... whenever
     Thou descendest into mortal flesh to lift the
     burden of the earth... I shall precede Thee. 
     I shall take birth upon the mortal soil. 
     I shall endure the walls of mortal palaces and
     the threats of arrogant kings. 
     And when Thou comest to claim Me, I shall look
     across the assembly of false suitors, bypass
     them all, and place my heart into Thy hands
     alone.
