# 🎬 SCREENPLAY SCENE 03: THE COSMIC ECLIPSE AND ASURA INVASION

**EXT. GATES OF AMARAVATI - NIGHT**

A terrifying, unnatural darkness has choked the heavens. 
The celestial spires, once crystalline and luminous, are now stained with cold grey grime. Dead leaves blow across shattered parapets.

ON THE RAMPARTS—

DEVA SENTRIES lean against their spears. Their skin is sallow, their knuckles trembling. The divine armor hangs loose on their emaciated frames.

BELOW, ON THE CELESTIAL PLAIN—

*BOOM.*

*BOOM.*

A rhythmic, ground-splitting percussion. Drums made from the hide of planetary beasts.

Through the black fog marches an army of TITANS.
THE ASURAS. Hundreds of thousands. Towering silhouettes in jagged obsidian armor, horned war helms, carrying massive spiked clubs and serrated cleavers.

Leading the phalanx:

ASURA EMPEROR BALI. 
Eight feet of raw, disciplined power. Clad in black mail with gold inlay. His eyes burn with cold, calculated intelligence. In his hands, he carries an obsidian greatsword.

Beside him ride NAMUCHI, SAMBARA, and KALANEMI—ferocious warlords thirsty for blood.

                    NAMUCHI
          (snarling)
     Look at the ramparts, Emperor! The cowards have
     snuffed their own torches! Their halos are gone!

                    BALI
     Their halos did not die. Their spirit rotted.
     Bring up the ram. Break their heaven.

*CRASH!*

A colossal iron ram, shaped like a horned dragon, smashes into the adamantite gates of Amaravati. 
The gates splinter like dry pine.

INSIDE THE COURTYARD—

INDRA stands desperately in the center, flanked by AGNI (pale, his flames barely flickering) and VAYU (gasping for breath).

                    INDRA
          (raising the Vajra with trembling arms)
     Hold the gates! Stand your ground, sons of
     Aditi! By the thunderbolt—

Indra swings the Vajra. 
A pathetic, sputtering blue spark crawls three feet into the air and DIES with a faint *fizz*.

Bali kicks the shattered gate doors inward. He steps into the courtyard alone, looking around with a sneer.

                    BALI
     Is this the thunderbolt that split mountains,
     Purandara?

Indra lunges forward with a spear. 
Bali doesn't even draw his sword. With one bare, gauntleted hand, he CATCHES the spearhead in mid-thrust.

*SNAP.*

Bali snaps the divine spear in half like a twig. With a casual backhand blow, he strikes Indra across the face.
Indra flies backward across the flagstones, spitting blood, his golden crown rolling through the dirt.

Asura warriors flood the square, dragging down the golden banners of heaven and replacing them with black-and-crimson standards.

Bali steps up to the fallen King of Heaven. He places the heel of his iron boot onto Indra’s chest, pressing down until the god wheezes.

                    BALI (CONT'D)
     You ruled the worlds by stolen light, Indra.
     Now the light has starved. Amaravati belongs to
     the children of Diti. Crawl into the void before
     I decide your head makes a poor trophy.

Bali steps off him and strides toward the throne hall.

Agni and Vayu drag the battered, weeping Indra backward into the shadows of the void.
