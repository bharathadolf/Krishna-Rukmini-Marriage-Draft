# 🎬 SCREENPLAY SCENE 05: VISHNU'S COUNSEL — THE ASYMMETRIC TRUCE

**EXT. SHORE OF KSHEERA SAGARA (OCEAN OF MILK) - TWILIGHT**

An endless horizon of liquid pearl. The waters do not crash—they roll in luminous, silent waves of warm white foam. 
The sky above is an infinite gradient of deep cosmic indigo and starlight.

ON THE SHORE—

Brahma and the ragged assembly of Devas stand knee-deep in the milky foam. 
Their voices rise together, chanting the ancient resonant verses of the PURUSHA SUKTA:

                    CHORUS OF DEVAS
     *Sahasra-śīrṣā puruṣaḥ sahasrākṣaḥ sahasrapāt...*
     *Sa bhūmiṁ viśvato vṛtvāty-atiṣṭhad-daśāṅgulam...*

IN THE DISTANCE—

The milky surface begins to shimmer with golden luminescence. 

OUT OF THE WATERS RISES—

LORD VISHNU. 
Colossal, serene, dark blue as a monsoon cloud heavy with rain. Clad in brilliant golden silks (*Pitambara*). Four arms holding the CONCH, the DISCUS, the MACE, and the LOTUS. 
His face possesses an absolute, timeless calm—a smile that carries the gentle irony of eternity.

Indra falls to his knees in the foam, gasping.

                    INDRA
     Lord! Save us! Strike the Asuras down from
     behind their walls! Unleash Thy Discus!

Vishnu raises His slender right hand. The gesture is small, yet it instantly stills the wind. 
His voice resonates not in the air, but directly inside the minds of all present.

                    LORD VISHNU
     Strike them, Indra? With what army? Thy warriors
     cannot hold a spear. 
     He who fights with anger against a stronger foe
     leaps into the furnace of his own destruction.

                    INDRA
     Then what can we do? Surrender?

                    LORD VISHNU
     Thou shalt master the craft of the asymmetric
     truce. Thou shalt make peace with Bali.

Indra looks up in horror.

                    INDRA
     Peace? With the butcher who stands upon my
     throne?

                    LORD VISHNU
     A treaty is not a surrender, Indra; it is the
     shield of the patient.
     Thou and the Asuras shalt unite to perform the
     greatest labor since the dawn of time: thou
     shalt churn this Ocean of Milk.

Brahma leans forward, his four faces alight with understanding.

                    BRAHMA
     The ocean... the repository of primordial seeds.

                    LORD VISHNU
     Within these white depths lie both Sri—the lost
     grace of the worlds—and Amrita, the nectar of
     undying life. 
     Thou dost not have the physical strength to
     stir this sea alone. Thou needest the titan
     sinews of the Asuras. Offer them equal share in
     the nectar.

                    INDRA
          (panicking)
     Equal share? If Bali drinks Amrita, his tyranny
     becomes immortal! We seal our own doom!

Vishnu steps lightly across the surface of the foam, coming to stand directly before Indra. 
His dark, lotus eyes pierce Indra’s soul with infinite depth.

                    LORD VISHNU
     Dost thou think the nectar will rise without
     labor? The sea will yield many things before the
     cup appears—some sweet, and some so bitter they
     will crack the teeth of the arrogant.
     Let the Asuras pull. Let them sweat. 
     Commit thy heart to Dharma, Indra, and leave the
     distribution of the fruit to Me. No crown won by
     raw ego can survive the touch of Grace.
