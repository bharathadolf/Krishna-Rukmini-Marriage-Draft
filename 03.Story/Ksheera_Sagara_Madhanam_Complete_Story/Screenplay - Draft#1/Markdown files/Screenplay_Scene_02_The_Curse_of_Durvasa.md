# 🎬 SCREENPLAY SCENE 02: THE CURSE OF DURVASA

**EXT. CROSSROADS OF THE HEAVENS - DAY**

The joyous celestial music cuts out abruptly. An uncanny, heavy silence smothers the avenue.

Standing in the center of the road, blocking the colossal bulk of Airavata, is—

SAGE DURVASA.

Matted locks like coiled black serpents. Skin smeared with grey funeral ash. Deep, hollow eyes that burn with the fierce, unshielded fire of Lord Shiva's Rudra energy. He wears a ragged deerskin across his sinewy shoulders.

In his calloused hands, he cradles a celestial GARLAND OF PARIJATA FLOWERS. 
The garland does not wither; it pulses with a warm, golden, breathing luminescence. Celestial bees hover over it in ecstatic trance. It is the raw, living embodiment of SRI—Divine Fortune and Auspiciousness.

Airavata comes to a halt. His massive ears flap once.

Indra looks down from the howdah, mildly annoyed at the interruption.

                    INDRA
          (condescending)
     Brahmarshi Durvasa. To what does heaven owe
     the honor of your austere presence?

Durvasa takes a step forward. His voice is deep, gravelly, vibrating through the marble bedrock.

                    DURVASA
     I bring no petition, Purandara. From the hands
     of the Divine Mother Herself, I received this
     garland of Sri. It is the essence of all fortune,
     all righteousness, all sovereignty. I bestow it
     upon thee, King of gods, as heaven’s sacred crown.

Durvasa holds the radiant garland upward.

Indra glances at it casually. Without descending from his howdah, without even setting down his wine goblet, he leans over the side and catches the garland with his LEFT hand.

                    INDRA
     A fragrant gift, Rishi. Its perfume is indeed
     sweet.

Indra sniffs it once. He finds the scent overpowering. With a careless flick of his wrist—

HE TOSSES THE SACRED GARLAND ONTO THE FOREHEAD OF AIRAVATA.

Durvasa’s eyes widen. A flicker of disbelief.

ON AIRAVATA—

The celestial bees swarming the flowers begin buzzing aggressively around the elephant’s sensitive eyes. 
Airavata snorts in irritation.

The colossal trunk curls upward. 

THE TRUNK RIPS THE GARLAND FROM HIS FOREHEAD. 

FLINGS IT DOWN ONTO THE DUSTY ROAD.

AND WITH A HEAVY, THUDDING STEP—

AIRAVATA’S MASSIVE GREY FOOT STOMPS DOWN DIRECTLY ONTO THE GARLAND.

*CRUNCH.* 

Petals burst. Golden divine light splatters like crushed fruit and extinguishes in the dirt.

TOTAL SILENCE.

The courtiers freeze. Color drains from Matali’s face.

Durvasa stares at the muddy pulp beneath the elephant’s foot. 

His staff begins to shake. His fingernails dig into his wooden rosary until it snaps. 
When he raises his head, his eyes are no longer human—they are TWO WELLS OF LIVING, WHITE-HOT FLAME.

                    DURVASA
          (voice cracking like thunder across the cosmos)
     THOU FOOL!

A shockwave of sheer ascetic heat rips across the promenade. The rainbow arches overhead shudder.

Indra nearly drops his goblet. For the first time, fear enters his eyes.

                    INDRA
          (stammering)
     Holy one... wait! It was the beast—it was
     Airavata’s instinct, not my design—

                    DURVASA
     THE BEAST OBEYS THE PRIDE OF ITS MASTER!
     Thou hast sat upon thy golden throne so long
     thou hast forgotten that sovereignty is not thy
     property—it is a loan granted by GRACE!
     And thou hast cast Grace into the dirt to be
     trampled underfoot!

Durvasa slams his staff onto the ground. A web of black cracks races through the gold pavement.

                    DURVASA (CONT'D)
     HEAR MY WORD, LORD OF HEAVEN! Since thou hast
     treated Sri as worthless weed, SRI SHALL ABANDON
     THEE! Thy realms shall wither! Thy radiance
     shall turn to ash! The Three Worlds shall rot
     beneath a sky stripped of fortune and courage!
     NISSHRIKA BHAVATU! THOU SHALT BE EMPTY!

Durvasa turns on his heel. His silhouette vanishes in a vortex of black ash.

IN THE AIR ABOVE AMARAVATI—

A sickening, low groaning sound. 
The golden sky suddenly pales. The blossoms on every celestial tree wilt, turning black and brittle. 
Indra looks down at his own hand. 

The divine golden light radiating from his skin flickers... and DIES.
