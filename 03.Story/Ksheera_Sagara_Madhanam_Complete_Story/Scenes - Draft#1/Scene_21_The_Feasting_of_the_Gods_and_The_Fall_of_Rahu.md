# 🎬 SCENE 21: THE FEASTING OF THE GODS AND THE FALL OF RAHU
### Phase: Act VII — The Struggle for Amrita & The Mohini Enchantment

---

## 📌 SCENE METADATA
* **Scene Heading:** `EXT. THE BANQUET PAVILION OF THE OCEAN - SUNSET`
* **Characters:** MOHINI, SURYA, CHANDRA, SVARBHANU (RAHU-KETU), DEVAS, ASURAS.
* **Tone:** Suspenseful, surgical, hypnotic, shocking.
* **Dramatic Goal:** Dramatize Mohini systematically feeding the Devas while enchanting the Asuras; Svarbhanu’s infiltration; the lethal strike of the Sudarshana Chakra, birthing Rahu and Ketu.

---

## 🎥 VISUAL & AUDIO GRAMMAR
* **Visual Style:** Crimson and gold sunset blazing across the white waves. Two parallel rows of seated warriors: the radiant Devas on the right, the rapt Asuras on the left. Mohini glides between them like a swan of living gold.
* **Camera Movement:** A tense, procedural tracking shot following the golden ladle dripping luminous Amrita into Deva chalices; sudden whip-pan and speed-ramp as the Sudarshana Chakra materializes in living flame.
* **Sound Design:** Hypnotic chiming music, the soft pour of nectar, the frantic whisper of Surya, followed by the deafening, razor-sharp sonic whistle of the spinning Discus.

---

## 📜 SCENE ACTION & BEATS

### Beat 1: The Two Rows
Mohini arranges the hosts in two separate, parallel lines:
* On the left: The Asuras sit motionless, their eyes locked on Mohini’s swaying hips, Her enchanting smiles, and Her teasing promises.
* On the right: The Devas sit with lowered eyes and cupped hands.
Mohini dances down the Deva row, systematically pouring the glowing Amrita into their hands. The Asuras sit smiling in drunken hypnosis, convinced She is saving the sweetest draught for them.

### Beat 2: The Infiltration of Svarbhanu
One Asura sees through the spell: **SVARBHANU**. 
Using dark sorcery, he shifts his form into that of an Aditya god and slips quietly into the Deva row, seating himself directly between **SURYA (the Sun)** and **CHANDRA (the Moon)**.
Mohini approaches. She tilts the golden urn, pouring a single drop of liquid immortality into Svarbhanu’s cupped palms. 
Svarbhanu brings his hands to his lips. The nectar enters his mouth.

### Beat 3: The Severing of Hubris
Just as the drop touches his throat:
SURYA and CHANDRA shout in horror: *"An impostor! It is Svarbhanu!"*
Mohini drops Her teasing facade in a microsecond. 
Her feminine form blazes with the terrifying, solar wrath of Lord Vishnu. The blazing, thousand-toothed **SUDARSHANA CHAKRA** manifests above Her hand and streaks across the hall like a laser of living fire.
*CLEAVE.* 
The Discus cleanly severs Svarbhanu’s neck before the nectar can pass into his torso.
Because the Amrita had touched his throat, neither half can perish:
* The head becomes the immortal shadow planet **RAHU**, roaring with eternal hunger.
* The serpent body becomes **KETU**.
Both are cast into the celestial spheres, forever hunting the Sun and Moon in eclipses.

---

## 🎭 KEY DIALOGUE

**SVARBHANU**  
*(throat severed, his floating head screaming into space)*  
Narayana! Thou hast cheated me! The nectar touched my lips! I am immortal!

**LORD VISHNU (MOHINI)**  
*(voice booming with cosmic authority)*  
Immortal in flesh, Svarbhanu, but devoid of light! Thou hast stolen the nectar of the gods, and as a shadow thou shalt crawl across the stars until the end of time!

## 🪞 THE MIRROR LAYER: BRIDGE TO *KRISHNA & RUKMINI*
* **Mortal Scene Counterpart:** `EXT. THE ROAD TO BHOJAKATA - SUNSET (DWAPARA YUGA)`
* **Character Mirror:** **The Slicing of Rahu-Ketu ↔ The Humiliation and Shaving of Prince Rukmi**.
* **Dramatic Parallel:**
  The surgical strike of the Sudarshana Chakra severing the arrogant deceiver directly mirrors the climax of the mortal pursuit. Prince Rukmi pursues Krishna’s chariot, swearing to kill Krishna and drag Rukmini back by her hair. Krishna turns, shatters Rukmi’s chariot, and pins the prince to the earth with his drawn blade. Rukmini begs for her brother’s life. Krishna spares his neck—just as Vishnu spared Svarbhanu’s ultimate death—but uses his curved dagger to cleanly shave off half of Rukmi’s hair and mustache, stripping the arrogant prince of his warrior honor forever.
* **Match-Cut Transition Hook:**
  The burning Sudarshana Chakra spinning across the hall to slice Rahu’s neck ➔ **MATCH-CUT TO** ➔ Krishna’s silver hunting knife flashing in the sunset as it slices off the locks of Prince Rukmi in the dust.
