# 🎬 SCENE 01: THE PRIDE OF AMARAVATI
### Phase: Act I — The Fall of the Cosmos

---

## 📌 SCENE METADATA
* **Scene Heading:** `EXT. AMARAVATI - CELESTIAL PROMENADE - DAY (SATYA YUGA)`
* **Characters:** INDRA (King of the Devas), AIRAVATA (Divine Four-Tusked Elephant), APSARAS, CELESTIAL COURTIERS.
* **Tone:** Blindingly opulent, arrogant, grand, sensory overload.
* **Dramatic Goal:** Establish the zenith of Deva power, Indra’s fatal hubris (*Ahamkara*), and the illusion of permanent imperial supremacy.

---

## 🎥 VISUAL & AUDIO GRAMMAR
* **Visual Style:** Saturated celestial golds, clouds tinged with liquid amber, crystalline palace spires floating in a boundless sky. Full 1.43:1 IMAX framing.
* **Camera Movement:** A sweeping, high-altitude aerial tracking shot descending through golden clouds to frame Indra perched high upon the jeweled howdah of Airavata.
* **Sound Design:** Trumpeting celestial fanfares, laughing Gandharva melodies, crystalline chimes, and the deep, rhythmic, earth-shaking tread of the colossal elephant.

---

## 📜 SCENE ACTION & BEATS

### Beat 1: The Imperial Procession
The golden avenue of Amaravati gleams under eternal celestial sunlight. 
INDRA (radiant, clad in diamond-studded breastplate, wearing the crown of the Three Worlds) reclines casually atop AIRAVATA. His eyes are heavy with celestial wine and centuries of uninterrupted worship. Around him, Apsaras toss parijata petals that dissolve into shimmering sparks before hitting the flagstones.

### Beat 2: The Intoxication of Supremacy
Courtiers sing verses praising Indra as the unyielding slayer of Vritra, the lord of thunder, the master of all realms. Indra raises a jeweled goblet, barely acknowledging their praise. To him, the universe is a machine built to sustain his pleasure. He points his vajra idly at a gathering storm cloud, commanding it to rain pearls upon his retinue. The gods laugh in sycophantic chorus.

---

## 🎭 KEY DIALOGUE

**INDRA**  
*(smirking, to his charioteer Matali)*  
Look upon these realms, Matali. The Asuras grovel in the dark gutters of Patala. The mortals bleed on their soil to offer us ghee. Who in the three worlds can unseat a king who commands the lightning itself?

**MATALI**  
*(bowing, hesitant)*  
None among mortals or demons, my Lord. Yet the sages warn that time itself is a wheel...

**INDRA**  
Time is a wheel that turns beneath the feet of my elephant!

## 🪞 THE MIRROR LAYER: BRIDGE TO *KRISHNA & RUKMINI*
* **Mortal Scene Counterpart:** `EXT. BHOJAKATA - PALACE COURTYARD - DAY (DWAPARA YUGA)`
* **Character Mirror:** **Indra ↔ Prince Rukmi**.
* **Dramatic Parallel:** 
  Just as Indra rides Airavata blind to cosmic reality, Prince Rukmi struts through his newly fortified capital of Bhojakata, gloating over the impending wedding alliance with Chedi and Magadha. Rukmi mocks the Yadavas as "rustic cowherds and sea-rats," entirely blind to the moral bankruptcy of selling his sister to Shishupala.
* **Match-Cut Transition Hook:**
  Close-up on Indra’s sneering face as he looks down from his elephant ➔ **MATCH-CUT TO** ➔ Close-up on Prince Rukmi’s contemptuous eyes looking down from his chariot at the Vidarbha royal gates.
