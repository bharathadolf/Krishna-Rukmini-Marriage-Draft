# 🎬 SCENE 07: THE UPROOTING OF MOUNT MANDARA
### Phase: Act III — The Gathering of Primordial Forces

---

## 📌 SCENE METADATA
* **Scene Heading:** `EXT. SLOPES OF MOUNT MANDARA - CONTINENTAL HORIZON - DAY`
* **Characters:** LORD VISHNU, GARUDA, INDRA, BALI, MILLIONS OF DEVAS & ASURAS.
* **Tone:** Colossal, crushing, catastrophic, awe-inspiring.
* **Dramatic Goal:** Dramatize the sheer physical impossibility of the cosmic enterprise; human and titan strength fails under the weight of creation until divine intervention rescues them.

---

## 🎥 VISUAL & AUDIO GRAMMAR
* **Visual Style:** Titanic scale. Mount Mandara is not a hill, but a golden-peaked continental massif piercing the upper stratosphere, cloaked in primeval forests and diamond strata.
* **Camera Movement:** Extreme wide pull-backs revealing millions of minuscule ant-like figures (gods and titans) straining against the planetary roots of the mountain; sudden vertigo-inducing downward pans as the mountain slips.
* **Sound Design:** The agonizing groaning of tectonic plates tearing free; millions of voices screaming in exertion; the thunderous avalanche of falling rock; followed by the whistling sonic boom of Garuda’s wings.

---

## 📜 SCENE ACTION & BEATS

### Beat 1: The Collective Straining
Devas and Asuras link arms, surrounding the base of MOUNT MANDARA. Veins bulge on titan foreheads; divine light strains in godlike sinews. With a cataclysmic roar that fractures continental shelves, they rip the mountain from the mantle of the earth.

### Beat 2: The Collapse
Midway across the scorched plains toward the Milk Ocean, the sheer crushing density of Mandara becomes unbearable. Arms snap; knees buckle. 
The mountain tilts. A scream of panic sweeps the ranks as Mandara drops like an asteroid. Tens of thousands of Devas and Asuras are crushed beneath falling granite cliffs. The enterprise is dead before it has even begun.

### Beat 3: The Golden Wings of Garuda
A golden shadow blots out the sun. The wings of GARUDA spread across the horizon, displacing clouds with hurricane force. Perched upon the eagle's shoulders is LORD VISHNU. 
With effortless grace, Vishnu reaches down with a single hand, catches the jagged peak of Mandara as if it were a toy pebble, and hefts it onto Garuda’s back. With a tender glance of divine grace, Vishnu revives the crushed warriors, and Garuda deposits the titanic mountain upright into the heart of the Ocean of Milk.

---

## 🎭 KEY DIALOGUE

**BALI**  
*(panting heavily, spitting blood onto the cracked rock)*  
Even our combined sinews cannot bear the weight of this rock! We are trying to turn the wheel of existence with mortal hands!

**INDRA**  
*(staring upward in shock)*  
Look above! The Golden Wings!

**LORD VISHNU**  
*(voice soothing the trembling earth)*  
What is impossible for pride is weightless for devotion. Mount Mandara is set. Prepare the living rope.
