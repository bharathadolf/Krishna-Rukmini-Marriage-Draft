# 🎬 SCENE 03: THE COSMIC ECLIPSE AND ASURA INVASION
### Phase: Act I — The Fall of the Cosmos

---

## 📌 SCENE METADATA
* **Scene Heading:** `EXT. GATES OF AMARAVATI - NIGHT`
* **Characters:** INDRA, AGNI, VAYU, ASURA EMPEROR BALI, ASURA WARLORDS, PANICKED DEVA TROOPS.
* **Tone:** Apocalyptic, brutal, crushing, chaotic.
* **Dramatic Goal:** Dramatize the instantaneous collapse of the Deva hegemony under Durvasa's curse, and the devastating military invasion of the Asuras.

---

## 🎥 VISUAL & AUDIO GRAMMAR
* **Visual Style:** The celestial gold of heaven decays into sickly greenish-gray. Clouds dry up into dust. The sky turns the color of bruised iron. Harsh, directional torchlight casts jagged shadows across crumbling ramparts.
* **Camera Movement:** Frenetic, handheld combat cinematography. Rapid pans from retreating Devas dropping their tarnished armor to the towering, disciplined silhouette of Bali’s Asura phalanxes breaking through the gates.
* **Sound Design:** The eerie, whistling wind of a dying atmosphere, the shattering of celestial crystal gates, the thunderous war cries of the Daityas, and the deafening clatter of bronze shields.

---

## 📜 SCENE ACTION & BEATS

### Beat 1: The Decay of Reality
Across the celestial realms, the curse manifests with horrific speed. Sacrificial fires on Earth sputter and die; trees in heaven wither, dropping dead gray leaves. The radiant golden halos behind the heads of Indra, Agni, and Vayu flicker out like blown candles. Their skin turns pale and wrinkled; their arms tremble under the weight of weapons they once swung effortlessly.

### Beat 2: The Ram at the Gates
A seismic rumble shakes the city. The massive adamantite gates of Amaravati buckle inward under the blows of an obsidian battering ram. Through the breach pour the ASURA WARRIORS—hulking, fierce, disciplined, armored in black steel and horned helms.

### Beat 3: The Emperor Steps Through
At the head of the vanguard strides ASURA EMPEROR BALI. Towering, regal, unyielding, his eyes burn with disciplined triumph. He carries a massive serrated blade. 
Indra tries to summon lightning—a pathetic, sputtering spark jumps from the tip of the Vajra and dies in the dirt. 
Bali catches Indra’s spear with a bare armored gauntlet, wrenches it from his grip, and casts the King of Heaven down into the dust.

---

## 🎭 KEY DIALOGUE

**BALI**  
*(looking down at Indra with cold disgust)*  
Is this the master of the Three Worlds? You rule not by virtue or sacrifice, Purandara, but by stolen light. And now your light has starved.

**INDRA**  
*(coughing blood, clutching his withered chest)*  
The cosmic law... will not suffer you to hold this throne, Bali...

**BALI**  
The cosmic law bows to strength. Amaravati is ours. Flee, gods of heaven. Crawl into the caves of the universe while you still have breath to beg.
