# 🎬 SCENE 05: VISHNU'S COUNSEL — THE ASYMMETRIC TRUCE
### Phase: Act II — The Great Conclave & The Primal Counsel

---

## 📌 SCENE METADATA
* **Scene Heading:** `EXT. SHORE OF KSHEERA SAGARA (OCEAN OF MILK) - TWILIGHT`
* **Characters:** LORD VISHNU, LORD BRAHMA, INDRA, DEVA HOSTS.
* **Tone:** Mystical, serene, stratospheric, transcendent.
* **Dramatic Goal:** Introduce Lord Vishnu; establish the strategic doctrine of the coalition with the enemy and the cosmic blueprint for churning the ocean.

---

## 🎥 VISUAL & AUDIO GRAMMAR
* **Visual Style:** An endless, luminescent white ocean of liquid pearl. Gentle waves roll without sound. The sky is a gradient of indigo and cosmic violet. 
* **Camera Movement:** A slow, hypnotic push-in across the milky waters toward a radiant golden light that crystallizes into the towering, four-armed form of LORD VISHNU, standing serene upon the waters, holding Conch, Discus, Mace, and Lotus.
* **Sound Design:** The deep, resonant sound of the ocean gently breathing; the distant celestial hum of the *Omkara*; Vishnu's voice resonates not from the air, but inside the skulls of the listeners.

---

## 📜 SCENE ACTION & BEATS

### Beat 1: The Manifestation
Brahma and the Devas stand at the shore, singing the ancient *Purusha Sukta*. The milky waters swirl, and LORD VISHNU appears. His blue complexion is the color of a monsoon raincloud filled with starlight; His yellow silks float on the ethereal breeze. His eyes are calm, infinite, amused.

### Beat 2: The Strategic Doctrine
Indra begs for a sudden miracle to destroy the Asuras. Vishnu raises a slender blue hand.
He lays down the counter-intuitive doctrine of supreme statecraft: *When the adversary is overwhelmingly superior in raw physical force, direct war is madness. One must enter into an asymmetric alliance.*

### Beat 3: The Grand Enterprise
Vishnu reveals the impossible task: They must churn the Ocean of Milk. Within its depths lie both *Sri* (the lost glory of the cosmos) and *Amrita* (the nectar of immortality). 
To churn an ocean requires titanic physical force—force the depleted Devas do not possess. They must invite their conquerors, the Asuras, to be equal partners in the labor.

---

## 🎭 KEY DIALOGUE

**INDRA**  
*(incredulous, trembling)*  
Alloy ourselves with Bali? With the slaughterers of our kin? Lord, they hold heaven by the throat! If we offer them immortality, we seal our eternal slavery!

**LORD VISHNU**  
*(with a serene, enigmatic smile)*  
He who would survive the storm must bend like the reed before the gale. Go to Bali. Treat him with honor. Speak to him of Amrita, for even the proudest tyrant thirsts to conquer death. 

**INDRA**  
And when the nectar rises? Bali’s legions will seize it!

**LORD VISHNU**  
Let them labor. Let them sweat. The ocean will yield many things before the nectar—some bitter as annihilation, some radiant as sovereignty. Keep your eyes on Dharma and leave the fruit of the labor to Me. No crown won by brute ego can long endure the judgment of Grace.
