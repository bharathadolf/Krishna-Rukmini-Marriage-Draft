## 2. Where We Stand: The Gap Analysis

### What We Have (The "World & Structure" Phase):
We possess an **industry-leading development package**. The world-building, mythological consistency, thematic clarity, character psychology, and high-level scene sequences are virtually 100% mapped. You know *what* happens, *why* it happens, and *how* different directors would shoot it.

### What Is Missing (The "Scriptwriting & Execution" Phase):
We do not yet have the **actual feature film screenplay**. In professional filmmaking, the transition from development notes to production script requires bridging four crucial gaps:

1. **Format Transition**: Moving from analytical and comparative markdown documents into **dramatic screenplay format** (Scene Sluglines, Action Blocks, Character Cues, Dialogue, Parentheticals).
2. **Beat Locking**: Committing to ONE definitive narrative spine (synthesizing the best elements of Models 1–7) rather than exploring seven alternative possibilities.
3. **Micro-Pacing & Scene Economy**: Breaking down the 16 sequences into **70–80 specific numbered scenes** with exact page allocations (1 page = ~1 minute of screen time = 120–135 pages total).
4. **Dialogue & Subtext**: Writing the living, breathing lines of dialogue, subtext, Sanskrit resonance, and character banter for actors to perform.

## 4. Granular Breakdown of Each Remaining Phase

### Phase 1: Locking the Creative Spine
- **Objective**: Consolidate the findings from `BUILD.md` and `Answers/` into a single, definitive creative mandate.
- **Key Decisions**:
  - *Tone*: Will the film adopt Nolan's temporal grandeur, Snyder's mythic weight, or Tarantino's energetic dialogue rhythm? (Recommended: A synthesis of **Nolan's structural elegance**, **Snyder's visual majesty**, and **Scorsese's emotional depth**).
  - *Prologue Choice*: Confirm the exact screen time allocation for the *Ksheera Sagara Madhanam* prologue (Recommended: 6–8 minutes).
  - *The Central Conflict*: Formalize the dramatic triangle: Krishna (Sovereignty/Bhakti) vs. Rukmi (Ego/Status) vs. Rukmini (Agency/Devotion).

### Phase 2: The Feature Film Prose Treatment (25–35 Pages)
- **Objective**: Write out the complete 150-minute story in engaging, cinematic prose before touching screenplay formatting software.
- **Why It Matters**: The treatment allows us to test character emotional arcs, verify scene pacing, and ensure thematic pay-offs without getting bogged down in formatting syntax. Every sequence from Act I to Act III is experienced as a continuous narrative journey.

### Phase 3: The Step Outline (70–80 Numbered Scenes)
- **Objective**: Transform the prose treatment into a rigorous mechanical blueprint for the writer.
- **Structure of Every Scene Entry**:
  - `SCENE NUMBER & SLUGLINE`: e.g., `SCENE 14 - INT. KUNDINA PALACE - BEDCHAMBER - NIGHT`
  - `CHARACTERS PRESENT`: Rukmini, Sudeva.
  - `SCENE OBJECTIVE`: Rukmini must convince Sudeva to risk his life by carrying the secret letter.
  - `OBSTACLE / RESISTANCE`: Sudeva is terrified of Rukmi's secret police and knows the penalty is impalement.
  - `THE EMOTIONAL BEAT / TURN`: From fear to spiritual awakening; Sudeva touches the wax seal and commits.
  - `PAGE TARGET`: 2.5 pages.

### Phase 4: Full Feature Screenplay First Draft (120–135 Pages)
- **Objective**: Draft the actual script pages following standard industry formatting:
  - **Act I (Pages 1–35)**:
    - The Primordial Churning prologue.
    - The dual realities of Dwarka and Kundina.
    - Rukmi usurping King Bhishmaka and forcing the alliance with Shishupala.
    - Rukmini carving the Seven Verses; Sudeva's midnight departure.
  - **Act II-A (Pages 35–70)**:
    - Sudeva's brutal trek across the salt flats.
    - Krishna receiving the messenger in Dwarka; the washing of the feet.
    - Krishna's lone departure; Balarama mobilizing the Yadava host.
    - The coalition armies of Jarasandha and Chedi massing around Kundina.
  - **Act II-B (Pages 70–95)**:
    - The eve of the wedding: contrasting vigils.
    - The bridal procession to the Ambika temple.
    - T-minus 1 hour countdown to high noon.
    - The breach of the perimeter; Krishna hoisting Rukmini onto the chariot before thousands of stunned warriors.
  - **Act III (Pages 95–130)**:
    - High-velocity vehicular chariot pursuit across the Vidarbha canyon.
    - Balarama's rearguard force intercepting the allied kings with thunderous power.
    - The climactic duel between Krishna and Rukmi; the snapping of the Vijaya bow.
    - The straight-razor shaving of Rukmi's crown and mustache; Rukmini pleading for his life.
    - The founding of Bhojakata in bitter dust vs. the glorious wedding triumph in Dwarka.
    - The cosmic flash-forward to Kurukshetra: Krishna as Parthasarathi, blowing the Panchajanya.

### Phase 5: Script Doctoring & Revision Passes
- **Pacing & Trimming Pass**: Cut unnecessary dialogue, ensure no scene starts late or leaves late.
- **Dialogue & Subtext Pass**: Ensure every character speaks in their unique cadence; remove on-the-nose exposition.
- **Sanskrit & Cultural Resonance Pass**: Integrate authentic sloka citations from *Srimad Bhagavatam* Canto 10 with cinematic English translations.

### Phase 6: Production Packaging
- **Pitch Deck**: Logline, thematic synopsis, character breakdown, visual tone board.
- **Lookbook**: Lighting references, architectural styles (Dwarka marble vs. Kundina bronze), costume designs, vehicle blueprints (The *Jaitra* chariot).
